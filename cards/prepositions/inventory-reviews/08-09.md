# Phase 2 independent audit — 08 Fixed Expressions and 09 Grammar Traps

Scope audited: `inventory-slices/08-09.tsv`, the integrated `coverage.tsv`, all
nine subdecks for routing/duplicate checks, the Phase 1 slice contract, and the
complete deck plan. This report does not modify inventory rows or generate cards.

Evidence checked: [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/on-short-notice)
confirms `on short notice` as the US form (with BrE `at short notice`), and its
[relative-pronoun grammar](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-pronouns/)
documents both neutral stranding and formal preposition-fronting. Cambridge also
records `under/in any circumstances` variation. [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/comprise)
documents that `be comprised of` is established, though usage-sensitive, and its
[usage note](https://www.merriam-webster.com/grammar/usage-notes) confirms that
sentence-final prepositions are grammatical.

## Blocking

None. The source remains structurally usable because every item is still
`proposed`, not exported.

## Major

### M1 — The formal fixed-expression tail is too large for an item-selective core

**Items:** `FIXED-CAUSE-AS-A-CONSEQUENCE-OF`, `FIXED-CAUSE-ON-ACCOUNT-OF`,
`FIXED-CAUSE-IN-VIEW-OF`, `FIXED-EXCEPTION-WITH-THE-EXCEPTION-OF`,
`FIXED-REFERENCE-AS-TO`, `FIXED-REFERENCE-IN-CONNECTION-WITH`,
`FIXED-REFERENCE-FOR-THE-PURPOSES-OF`, `FIXED-RELATION-IN-CONJUNCTION-WITH`,
`FIXED-RELATION-IN-ASSOCIATION-WITH`, `FIXED-AGENCY-IN-THE-NAME-OF`,
`FIXED-AGENCY-ON-THE-PART-OF`, `FIXED-METHOD-BY-MEANS-OF`,
`FIXED-GOAL-WITH-THE-AIM-OF`, `FIXED-GOAL-IN-PURSUIT-OF`,
`FIXED-BENEFIT-IN-THE-INTERESTS-OF`, and `FIXED-STANCE-FROM-THE-STANDPOINT-OF`.

**Consequence:** These rows mostly duplicate a more neutral, already-included
decision (`because of`, `in light of`, `except for`, `regarding`, `in relation
to`, `in collaboration with`, `on behalf of`, `by`, `for the purpose of`, or
`from my perspective`). Together they turn 08 into a thesaurus-like formal list,
contrary to the plan's item-selective and broadly useful formal-expression scope.

**Action: exclude.** Retain the neutral/core alternatives just named. For each
listed row, keep its existing fields but set `status=excluded` and set
`notes=Excluded in Phase 2: lower-value formal synonym; covered by [core item].`

### M2 — `comprised of` must not be taught as simply wrong

**Item:** `TRAP-DIRECT-COMPRISE`.

**Consequence:** The active pattern `The report comprises five sections` is a
good formal C1 pattern, but a repair card whose implied correction is
`is comprised of -> comprises` would teach a disputed prohibition. Merriam-Webster
records passive `be comprised of` as established and now more frequent in general
use, while noting that some editors still object.

**Action: edit.** Replace these fields:

| Field | Replacement |
|---|---|
| `target_form` | `comprise + parts; be comprised of + parts` |
| `meaning_or_distinction` | `uses active comprise for a whole that includes parts; recognizes be comprised of as common but usage-sensitive` |
| `construction` | `whole + comprises + parts; whole + is comprised of + parts` |
| `confusables` | `comprise of; consist of; be composed of` |
| `card_type` | `contrast` |
| `notes` | `Do not mark be comprised of ungrammatical. Prefer consist of or be composed of where a conservative formal style is required.` |

### M3 — `where ... at` cannot be a repair whose neutral rewrite is treated as a correction

**Item:** `TRAP-QUESTION-WHERE-AT`.

**Consequence:** Locative `Where are you at?` is informal/conversational AmE;
the separate idiom `where someone is at` is also standard informal. A `repair`
card invites the learner to treat the form as wrong, violating the plan's
alternative-answer rule.

**Action: edit.** Replace these fields:

| Field | Replacement |
|---|---|
| `target_form` | `Where are you? / Where are you at?` |
| `meaning_or_distinction` | `uses Where are you? as neutral AmE; recognizes Where are you at? as conversational and regionally variable, not ungrammatical` |
| `construction` | `where + be + subject; conversational where + be + subject + at` |
| `confusables` | `treating sentence-final at as an error` |
| `card_type` | `contrast` |
| `notes` | `Do not use a repair prompt. Keep the locative and the idiom where someone is at distinct in examples.` |

### M4 — Five grammar-trap rows are not yet atomically testable

**Items:** `TRAP-COMPLEMENT-PREPOSITION-NP`,
`TRAP-COMPLEMENT-PREPOSITION-VING`, `TRAP-PARTICLE-SEPARABLE`,
`TRAP-PREPOSITIONAL-VERB-NONSEPARABLE`, and
`TRAP-PARTICLE-INTRANSITIVE-VS-PREP`.

**Consequence:** Each is a general grammar label, not one answer a learner can
retrieve without being told the lexical carrier. A Front for `preposition + noun
phrase` or `verb + preposition + object` would violate the one-decision and
no-broad-label rules. The broad V-ing row also overlaps the concrete `without`,
`before/after`, and prepositional-`to` rows.

**Action: edit or exclude.** Retain the concept in a note rather than making it
the target form. Do not create a duplicate 09 card for a fixed expression whose
one home is already 08.

| Item | Replacement `target_form` | `construction` | `card_type` | `notes` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `TRAP-COMPLEMENT-PREPOSITION-NP` | **Exclude; retain FIXED-CAUSE-BECAUSE-OF in 08** | — | — | `A generic NP-complement label is not a card and because of already has the correct home.` |
| `TRAP-COMPLEMENT-PREPOSITION-VING` | **Exclude; retain TRAP-COMPLEMENT-WITHOUT-VING** | — | — | `The generic claim is not atomically testable; do not create a duplicate without + V-ing decision.` |
| `TRAP-PARTICLE-SEPARABLE` | `turn on the light / turn the light on` | `turn + particle + noun; turn + noun + particle` | `contrast` | `Use one separable carrier; do not claim all particles are separable.` |
| `TRAP-PREPOSITIONAL-VERB-NONSEPARABLE` | `rely on + noun` | `rely + preposition + object` | `repair` | `Cross-reference the 05 anchor; contrast only the placement decision.` |
| `TRAP-PARTICLE-INTRANSITIVE-VS-PREP` | `go out / go out of + place` | `go out; go out of + noun` | `contrast` | `Use the same lexical carrier so the object requirement is observable.` |

For `TRAP-COMPLEMENT-PREPOSITION-VING`, the cleaner integration is **exclude**
the generic row and retain `TRAP-COMPLEMENT-WITHOUT-VING`; do not make two cards
for the same `without + V-ing` decision.

### M5 — `under certain circumstances` advertises a non-existent exclusive choice

**Item:** `FIXED-CONDITION-UNDER-CERTAIN-CIRCUMSTANCES`.

**Consequence:** The row itself says `in certain circumstances` is natural. A
single-answer `chunk` for `under` cannot fairly test the form, and it adds little
beyond the stronger fixed negative `under no circumstances`.

**Action: exclude.** Set `status=excluded` and
`notes=Excluded in Phase 2: in/under certain circumstances overlap; no stable retrieval contrast. Retain under no circumstances.`

### M6 — `to a certain degree` duplicates the same degree decision

**Item:** `FIXED-DEGREE-TO-A-CERTAIN-DEGREE`.

**Consequence:** It does not create a meaning or complement decision beyond
`FIXED-DEGREE-TO-SOME-EXTENT`; a second card would add repetition rather than
coverage.

**Action: exclude.** Set `status=excluded` and
`notes=Excluded in Phase 2: duplicate partial-degree decision; retain to some extent.`

## Minor

### m1 — Mixed accepted alternatives need learner-facing handling, not a forced blank

**Items:** `FIXED-COMPARISON-IN-COMPARISON-WITH-TO` and
`FIXED-CONTRAST-IN-CONTRAST-TO-WITH`.

**Consequence:** Both alternatives are normal in many contexts. A `chunk` card
can display them, but a blank-form Front would be unfair if it expects only one.

**Action: keep with edit.** Keep the IDs and replace `notes` with:
`Use a completed chunk card or put Also OK on the Back; do not make a blank that rejects the other listed form.`

### m2 — `according to me` needs a narrower repair claim

**Item:** `FIXED-REFERENCE-ACCORDING-TO-ME`.

**Consequence:** The current caveat is sound, but the target may still prompt an
overbroad “wrong” judgment. It is normally unsuitable for stating one's current
opinion, not impossible in every metalinguistic or reported-opinion context.

**Action: keep with edit.** Replace `meaning_or_distinction` with:
`uses in my view/from my perspective for a current personal stance; uses according to + source to attribute information, including a person's reported position.`

### m3 — Preserve the verified AmE short-notice distinction

**Item:** `FIXED-TIME-ON-SHORT-NOTICE`.

**Consequence:** No defect found. Cambridge labels `on short notice` US and `at
short notice` UK. Removing the regional note would create a false cross-variety
rule.

**Action: keep.** Existing fields and note are adequate.

### m4 — Sentence-final prepositions and relative-clause stranding are correctly routed, but repair wording must not call them errors

**Items:** `TRAP-QUESTION-STRANDED-PREPOSITION`,
`TRAP-RELATIVE-STRANDED-PREPOSITION`, `TRAP-RELATIVE-FRONTED-PREPOSITION`,
`TRAP-RELATIVE-NO-PREP-THAT`, and `TRAP-RELATIVE-OMITTED-PRONOUN`.

**Consequence:** Cambridge supports neutral stranding, formal fronting with
`whom/which`, prohibition of `preposition + that`, and omission only in defining
object relatives. The first item is typed `repair`, however, despite describing
a grammatical construction.

**Action: edit `TRAP-QUESTION-STRANDED-PREPOSITION`.** Set
`card_type=contrast` and `notes=Teach neutral stranded question beside formal fronting; never repair a sentence-final preposition merely because it is final.` Keep the remaining four rows.

### m5 — No high-value Section 4.8 or 4.9 family is missing

**Items:** none.

**Consequence:** Cause/result, concession, addition/exception, substitution,
reference/scope, response, agency, conformity/basis, stance, practical/theory,
conditions/time, direct-object traps, required complements, prepositional `to`,
placement, pronouns, and particle boundaries all have at least one viable row.

**Action: add 0.** Prefer pruning and making the existing rows concrete over
adding more lexical inventory.
