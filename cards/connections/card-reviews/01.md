## Verdict

but the semantic failure rate is high under the plan's exact printed-sentence
test. The strict source lint passes:


  rows; and

The vocabulary level is generally useful for IELTS 7/C1, and the Backs are

quality: it is that many context blanks retrieve one writer-selected phrase
even though another normal preposition, zero form, multiword phrase, or lexical
substitution fits the exact Front.
