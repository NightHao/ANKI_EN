# Independent audit — 08 Coordination and Parallelism / 09 Comparison, Degree, and Result Patterns

## Verdict

Structurally valid and largely plan-complete, but **not ready for card authoring
without edits**. The main defects are generic labels posing as atomic repairs,
and several contrast rows whose alternatives overlap unless a narrowly bounded
context is supplied. No proposed row should yet become `validated`.

## Must fix before authoring

1. **Generic parallelism rows are not closed-answer decisions:**
   `COORD-PARALLEL-NP`, `COORD-PARALLEL-ADJP`, `COORD-PARALLEL-PP`, and
   `COORD-PARALLEL-CLAUSE` say only “make the units parallel.” A Front could
   have many legitimate repairs, so its target is not objectively gradable.
   Exclude/defer these four broad labels, retaining concrete carriers
   (`COORD-PARALLEL-VING`, `COORD-PARALLEL-TO-INF`,
   `COORD-LIST-CONSISTENT-HEADS`, and the correlative placement rows). Add a
   concrete carrier only when an actual remaining learner error is identified.

2. **`COORD-ELLIPSIS-AMBIGUOUS-SCOPE` is a writing principle, not one item.**
   Its target permits any repeated material and therefore has no single
   answer. Exclude it, or split it into one lexical/structural minimal pair
   where repeating a preposition or auxiliary demonstrably changes scope.
   The existing `COORD-REPEATED-PREPOSITION-SCOPE` and
   `COORD-REPEATED-AUXILIARY-CONTRAST` already supply those concrete homes.

3. **Nearest-subject agreement needs an explicit safe authoring boundary.**
   `COORD-EITHER-SUBJECT-AGREEMENT` and
   `COORD-NEITHER-SUBJECT-AGREEMENT` correctly describe a common AmE rule,
   but a mixed-number compound subject often sounds awkward and is commonly
   recast. Change `card_type` to `context`; add: “Use only singular/singular
   or plural/plural production examples, or make the nearer-noun contrast
   explicit and accept recasting.” Do not make a bare correction blank that
   marks other standard editorial choices wrong.

4. **Do not force pragmatic boundary pairs as categorical grammar.**
   `CMP-NO-MORE-THAN` / `CMP-NOT-MORE-THAN` and
   `CMP-NO-LESS-THAN` / `CMP-NOT-LESS-THAN` share their truth-conditional
   limits (`at most` / `at least`); the difference is often emphasis and
   register, not a reliably recoverable blank. Merge each pair into one
   context/chunk row with an expectation-setting example, or retain both only
   as recognition contexts that explicitly accept the counterpart. Their
   current reciprocal `contrast` form creates false exclusivity.

5. **`CMP-TO-POINT-THAT` / `CMP-TO-POINT-WHERE` cannot be a forced binary.**
   Both can introduce a result or reached stage in overlapping contexts. Change
   both from `contrast` to `chunk`/`context`; write distinct contexts (explicit
   proposition for *that*, developed limit/stage for *where*) and say the
   alternative may be possible. `CMP-TO-POINT-WHERE` should use
   `finite-clause` rather than `relative-clause` in this module: the learner
   target is a degree/result continuation, not a Subdeck-06 noun-modifying
   relative lesson.

6. **`CMP-THAN-OBJECT-PRONOUN` overstates a choice.**
   *than me* is normal neutral AmE, while *than I am* is a finite-clause form;
   neither is a simple correction of the other. Change it to a chunk/context,
   name `than I am` (not bare `than I`) as the finite-clause alternative, and
   require an intended clause predicate before testing it.

## Should fix

- `COORD-SHARED-THAT` is too broad to test as written. Keep it as a context
  pattern only with one reporting verb and two content clauses; omission of
  the second *that* is normally optional, not a repair target.
- `COORD-RATHER-THAN-VING` should not say a finite-clause reading must be
  unavailable. Its valid closed lesson is simply parallel V-ing alternatives;
  bare-inf and to-inf are separate rows selected by their governing frame.
- `COORD-AS-WELL-AS-AGREEMENT` and `COORD-ALONG-WITH-AGREEMENT` should state
  that the supplement is parenthetical for agreement, but avoid examples where
  punctuation/proximity creates a second independent decision.
- `CMP-COMPARATIVE-TIMES` should say that `N times as + adjective/adverb + as`
  is the selected precise production form; do not imply that all uses of
  `N times more than` are wrong. Its existing deferred warning is appropriate.
- `CMP-SO-MANY-THAT` and `CMP-SO-MUCH-THAT` are form-selection contrasts, not
  necessarily repairs. A bounded count/mass prompt can keep `repair`, but
  otherwise use `contrast` so a grammatical sentence is not mislabeled wrong.
- `CMP-TOO-NOT-DOUBLE-NEGATIVE` needs one exact repair carrier; otherwise the
  special *too careful not to...* exception makes the broad target unsafe.

## Missing high-value families

1. Add **comparative/equality clauses**: `as + adjective/adverb + as + finite
   clause` and `not as/so + adjective/adverb + as + finite clause`. Existing
   NP rows and `than`-clause rows do not cover this common productive shape.
2. Add **multiplicative quantity**: `N times as much + mass noun + as` and
   `N times as many + plural noun + as` (or one tightly bounded contrast).
   The slice has adjective multiplicatives and correctly defers ambiguous
   *N times more than*, but lacks the precise high-value noun forms.
3. Add a concrete **shared-subject coordination** item only if the authoring
   audit finds `COORD-SHARED-PREDICATE-NO-COMMA` insufficient; do not add a
   generic word/phrase/clause taxonomy card.

## Duplicate / merge candidates and safe exclusions

- Exclude `COORD-PARALLEL-BARE-INF` as currently marked; it is covered by
  `COORD-SHARED-AUXILIARY`.
- The four generic parallelism rows and `COORD-ELLIPSIS-AMBIGUOUS-SCOPE` are
  safe exclusions after the concrete rows above remain.
- Preserve `COORD-AND-OR-DEFERRED`, `CMP-SO-ADJ-A-NOUN-DEFERRED`, and
  `CMP-TIMES-MORE-AMBIGUOUS-DEFERRED` as deferred recognition decisions; they
  are grammatical/register-sensitive but low-priority or ambiguous for active
  production.
- Preserve routing exclusions `CMP-DIFFERENT-FROM-REROUTED` and
  `CMP-PREFER-RATHER-THAN-REROUTED`. `COORD-RATHER-THAN-*` is the correct
  one-home location for non-verb-selected parallel alternatives; `as opposed
  to` remains correctly in 09 when the comparison frame is the lesson.
