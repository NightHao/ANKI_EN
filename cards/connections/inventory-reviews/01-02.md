# Independent audit — 01 Prepositions and Prepositional Phrases / 02 Verb Patterns

## Verdict

The slice is structurally sound and has broad IELTS-7/general-English coverage,
but is **not ready for card authoring**. Its principal problems are a few
one-home routing violations and broad rows that cannot produce one closed-answer
card. No proposed row should yet be promoted to `validated`.

## Must fix before authoring

1. **Reroute clause-linker decisions out of 01.**
   `PREP-TIME-WHILE-CLAUSE` and `PREP-TIME-BY-THE-TIME` have a finite-clause
   linker as their central decision. Under the plan's routing order they belong
   to 05, not 01. Exclude them here with a routing note and create/retain the
   active 05 rows. `PREP-TIME-BEFORE-AFTER` may remain only for NP/V-ing;
   remove its “clause variant” authoring implication.

2. **Split the broad institutional/site viewpoint row.**
   `PREP-PLACE-INSTITUTION` combines *at work*, *in school*, and *in the
   office*, which have different article, AmE enrollment, activity, and
   interior-viewpoint rules. It cannot be a single closed answer. Replace it
   with atomic, contextualized rows: at work; in school (enrolled); at/in the
   office (activity vs interior). Keep `PREP-DEFER-INSTITUTION-VARIETY` for
   hospital/prison variation rather than treating it as a universal rule.

3. **Split semantic bundles with independent answers.**
   `PREP-MOVE-AROUND` combines avoiding an obstacle and moving within an area;
   split into separate path rows. `PREP-ABSTRACT-CAUSE` combines *from* source
   cause and *through* causal process; split. `PREP-ABSTRACT-COMPARISON`
   combines like, unlike, and role *as*; separate comparison (*like/unlike*)
   from role/function (*as + NP*). Each current row needs different context
   and has more than one legitimate completion.

4. **Do not repair grammatical question stranding.**
   `PREP-STRANDING-QUESTION` must be `contrast`/context, not a repair: neutral
   AmE *Who did you speak to?* is grammatical. If formal fronting is included,
   test `To whom did you speak?` as a register alternative and never label the
   stranded form an error.

5. **Split verb rows that explicitly contain multiple senses/particles.**
   `VERB-PREP-LOOK` contains *look at*, *look for*, and *look after* and must
   become three rows. `VERB-PREP-RELATE` combines connection and identification
   senses; split. `VERB-PREP-WITHDRAW` combines leaving an activity and taking
   money from an account; split. These are not variations a single Anki grade
   can represent.

6. **Narrow `VERB-PREP-AGREE` before it becomes active.**
   Its current deferred status is right, but the eventual forms require at
   least separate decisions: *agree with + person/opinion*, *agree on +
   decision*, and *agree to + proposal*. Do not turn the triple into one
   contrast card.

## Should fix

- `PREP-PLACE-TRANSPORT` needs an explicit exception policy: *on a plane/train/
  bus* versus *in a car* is useful, but “in a bus” can be natural when the
  interior is salient. Use viewpoint contexts and do not repair it.
- `PREP-PLACE-ABOVE-OVER` and `PREP-PLACE-BELOW-UNDER` should be chunks or
  carefully bounded contexts, not blanket oppositions: *over/above* and
  *under/below* overlap outside contact/covering contexts.
- `PREP-MOVE-ALONG-PAST` should split if the Front ever asks for a preposition;
  route-following and landmark-passing are two different retrieval decisions.
- `PREP-ABSTRACT-TOPIC` must not make *about* versus *on* categorical. Keep
  *on* as common in formal/issue-focused contexts and accept overlap.
- `PREP-ABSTRACT-PURPOSE` needs one explicitly parallel minimal pair; otherwise
  a learner can reasonably choose *for + NP* as well as the stated
  *for + V-ing / to-inf* contrast.
- `VERB-PREP-COMPARE` should become a context/chunk rather than a hard
  with/to contrast: contemporary usage overlaps, although analysis versus
  likeness can guide examples.
- `VERB-PREP-CONSIST` should not contrast *consist of* with bare *comprise*
  as if the latter were only a confusable; make the whole/parts direction
  explicit and accept *be composed of* where a card presents it.
- `VERB-TO-USED` is learner-critical, but its *be used to + V-ing* half is an
  adjective/predicative construction. Retain one contrast only if integration
  explicitly grants this exception; otherwise 02 keeps *used to + bare-inf*
  and 03 owns the adjective-selected form.
- `VERB-PARTICLE-TURN-ON` and `VERB-PARTICLE-PICK-UP` are good concrete repair
  carriers. Keep their repair prompt to pronoun placement only, not general
  separability claims.

## Missing high-value families

1. Add a clear **preposition + pronoun case** contrast only if
   `PREP-COMPLEMENT-PRONOUN` is not already a concrete carrier: *between you
   and me*, not subject pronouns after a preposition. Do not retain a generic
   “prepositions take NP” label.
2. Add a split **verb + preposition recipient/content** row for *explain
   something to someone* if it is absent after reporting-family integration;
   the current `VERB-OBJECT-EXPLAIN` direct-object trap and
   `VERB-REPORT-EXPLAIN-WH` do not cover the recipient placement.
3. Consider one high-value **verb + object + bare-inf perception/causative
   passive boundary** only if the final deck needs it: *was made to leave*
   versus active *made her leave*. It must be a focused voice/complement card,
   not a general passive rule.

## Duplicate / merge candidates and safe exclusions/deferred rows

- Preserve `PREP-EXCLUDED-GENERIC-NP` and `VERB-EXCLUDED-GENERIC-VING` as
  visible exclusions: generic grammatical labels are not card targets.
- Preserve `PREP-DEFER-INSTITUTION-VARIETY` and `VERB-DEFER-AGREE-TRIPLE` as
  deferred until they are split and given contexts.
- `PREP-MOVE-ZERO-HOME` and `PREP-MOVE-ARRIVE` are complementary, not
  duplicates; preserve the explicit *arrive home* zero-preposition exception.
- Keep one combined `PREP-TIME-ON-TIME` / `PREP-TIME-IN-TIME` contrast only if
  one bounded schedule-versus-deadline scenario is used. Two reciprocal cards
  would duplicate the same decision.
- The direct-object rows (`VERB-OBJECT-DISCUSS`, `ENTER`, `REACH`, `APPROACH`,
  `CONTACT`, etc.) are appropriately anchored concrete traps and should not be
  merged into a generic “no preposition after verb” card.
