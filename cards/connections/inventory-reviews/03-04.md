# Phase 2 independent audit — 03 Adjective/Participle and 04 Noun Patterns

Scope: `inventory-slices/03-04.tsv` against the full master plan, slice contract, migration input, and current companion slices. This report does not alter inventory rows or author cards.

## Must-fix findings

### M1 — `likely to` is raising, not subject control

**Items:** `ADJ-068`, `ADJ-069`.

`ADJ-068` requires a “subject-as-doer” context. That is false: *The economy is likely to recover* and *The bill is likely to pass* are core patterns although neither subject is an intentional doer. Replace it with: “the main-clause subject is also the semantic subject of the infinitive; it need not be an agent.” Change `ADJ-069` to `It is likely that + clause` (or `It seems likely that + clause`), not incomplete `likely that + clause`. Cambridge documents both and calls the `that` form less common.

### M2 — The required content-`that` versus relative-`that` contrast is absent

**Item:** `NOUN-070`.

`claim which + relative clause` is not the relevant alternative. Replace it with the plan's same-marker contrast: `the claim that prices fell` (content) versus `the claim that they rejected` (relative). Use a two-sentence context card. The home remains 04; 06 only cross-references it.

### M3 — Source/target noun systems are split into non-closed rows

**Items:** `NOUN-004` + `NOUN-066`; `NOUN-005` + `NOUN-067`.

Each pair is one required contrast: `effect of + cause / effect on + target`, and the parallel `impact` pair. Separate `contrast` rows leave each blank open. Merge each pair into one two-context contrast and exclude or map the redundant IDs.

### M4 — `change of + amount` is not a stable general counterpart

**Item:** `NOUN-018` (and its contrast with `NOUN-017`).

`change of + amount` cannot share the simple increase/decrease variable-versus-amount lesson. *A change of policy/address* commonly means replacement, while *a change in policy* means alteration within a domain. Exclude/defer `NOUN-018` unless a narrow natural countable construction is selected; retain `NOUN-017` with a variable context.

### M5 — `opportunity for` conflates two constructions

**Item:** `NOUN-025` (with `NOUN-024`).

`opportunity for + person/activity` conflates an NP complement with the common `opportunity for + NP + to-inf` pattern; bare *opportunity for students* is not the productive action alternative to *opportunity to speak*. Split or rewrite as `opportunity for + NP + to-inf`, recording `NP; to-inf`, and make actor/action visible in the context.

### M6 — `advantage to + V-ing` is wrongly prohibited

**Item:** `NOUN-043` (with `NOUN-042`).

The note “Do not use advantage to + V-ing” is false. *There are advantages to online shopping* is standard. Add `V-ing-clause` to `NOUN-043` or create a context-bounded row; do not treat `of` and `to` as exclusive. Oxford lists both `advantage of (doing) something` and `advantage to (doing) something`.

### M7 — Two routing notes contradict the one-home order

**Items:** `NOUN-003`, `NOUN-012`.

`NOUN-003` labels noun *result of* as a contrast with verb *result in*, whose one home is 02 (`VERB-PREP-RESULT`). Make `NOUN-003` a noun chunk/context and cross-reference 02. `NOUN-012` properly excludes noun *answer for*, but says reroute to “05 Verb + Preposition”; correct this to **02 Verb Patterns**. The current 01–02 slice has no `answer for`, so add/reroute it there or preserve it as a visible migration gap.

## Should-fix findings

- **`ADJ-080`:** retain the required easy/hard contrast, but make `for + NP + to-inf` and both subject roles explicit. Use bounded contexts: *This manual is easy to use* versus *It is easy for new staff to use this manual*.
- **`ADJ-072`:** revise “pleased about the action” to a reliable event/stance reading, such as *pleased to announce/hear/meet*. `pleased with + NP` evaluates an object/result; do not make an unbounded blank.
- **`NOUN-001`:** change `reason why` from `finite-clause` to `wh-clause`; exemplify all three forms with article/definiteness cues.
- **`NOUN-013`/`014` and `NOUN-015`/`016`:** merge each variable/amount pair into one two-context contrast. The plan treats each as one decision.
- **`NOUN-024`/`025`, `NOUN-079`, `NOUN-080`:** use complete complements in prompts. In particular, *way to reduce costs* and *way of reducing costs* often overlap; make `NOUN-080` an acceptance-aware completed chunk or defer it.
- **`NOUN-040`/`041`, `NOUN-051`/`052`, `NOUN-028`/`029`:** retain as contrasts only when analytical/likeness, power/management, or evaluated-entity/topic contexts make the boundary observable. Otherwise they are overlap chunks.
- **`ADJ-020`, `ADJ-032`, `ADJ-037`, `ADJ-044`–`047`, `ADJ-049`, `ADJ-051`:** enforce their accepted-variant notes at authoring. Use completed chunks or `Also OK:`, never blanks that reject `skilled in`, `different than`, informal AmE `based off`, reaction `at/by`, or person-directed `with/at` alternatives.

## Missing high-value families

1. **`worth + V-ing`** — productive adjective-selected complement and a common *worth to do* transfer error. Add one 03 `repair` or bounded contrast row.
2. **A card-ready content/relative-`that` family** — nominally present as `NOUN-070`, but still missing until M2 is fixed.
3. **`answer for + NP/V-ing` migration** — a 02 verb frame, not new 04 content, but it needs a visible destination before integration.

Do not add a generic countability module. Make countability visible only where it closes the construction: *a/the reason*, *an increase of*, and uncountable *access* and *evidence*.

## Evidence

- The master plan requires `likely`, easy/hard, content-versus-relative `that`, reason, source/target, and variable/amount families (Sections 5.3–5.4), plus one-home and closed-answer constraints (Sections 6, 7.2, and 9).

- [Oxford Learner's Dictionaries on advantage](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/advantage_1) lists both `advantage of (doing) something` and `advantage to (doing) something`.
