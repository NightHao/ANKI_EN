# 03 Adjective and Participle Patterns

## C03-001

<!-- card
item: ADJ-001
type: chunk
status: validated
-->

### Front

`interested ___ + topic or activity`

### Back

Use **interested in + noun/V-ing** for something a person wants to learn about
or take part in.

- Several graduates are **interested in working in renewable energy**.
- I became **interested in urban planning** after attending a public hearing.

## C03-002

<!-- card
item: ADJ-002
type: contrast
status: validated
-->

### Front

Complete the matched pair.

1. Maya is **interested** ___ the ethics of artificial intelligence.
   (Maya feels interest)
2. The ethical debate is **interesting** ___ Maya. (the debate causes Maya's
   interest)

### Back

1. Maya is **interested in the ethics of artificial intelligence**.
2. The ethical debate is **interesting to Maya**.

Use `person + be interested in + topic` for the experiencer's feeling. Use
`thing + be interesting to + person` for the thing that causes the interest.

## C03-007

<!-- card
item: ADJ-008
type: chunk
status: validated
-->

### Front

Give the neutral topic default and the accepted field-oriented alternatives:

`knowledgeable ___ + topic or field`

### Back

Use **knowledgeable about + topic** as the neutral general-purpose form.
**Knowledgeable in + field/area** is natural for expertise within a field,
and **knowledgeable of + topic** is also accepted.

- The adviser is highly **knowledgeable about international tax law**.
- The new coordinator is **knowledgeable in several areas of environmental
  policy**.

**Also OK:** `knowledgeable of the area's flood history`.

## C03-008

<!-- card
item: ADJ-010
type: context
status: validated
-->

### Front

Complete the noun and embedded-question frames. Give both normal answers
where the prompt asks for two.

1. The board is not yet **certain** ___ the long-term cost of the proposal.
   (two normal answers)
2. Are you **certain** ___ which data set the report used? (neutral default)

### Back

1. The board is not yet **certain about/of the long-term cost** of the
   proposal.
2. Are you **certain about which data set the report used**?

**Certain about** works naturally before a noun or embedded question.
**Certain of** overlaps strongly before a noun. **Certain that + clause**
states a complete proposition.
