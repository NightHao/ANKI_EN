## Context for the newly added words

- **sink**: the basin where you wash your hands or dishes; also a verb meaning go below a surface.
- **showerhead**: the fitting that sprays shower water.
- **handle**: the part you hold or move to operate something; includes a shower handle. This broadly useful word is the headword.
- **knob**: a round control you turn; includes a shower knob. A knob is one possible form of a handle/control.
- **pee**: casual noun for urine or verb for urinating.
- **urinal**: the fixture used for urinating, not the urine itself.
- **toilet stall**: the enclosed compartment around a toilet in a public restroom; also called a bathroom stall or simply a stall.
- **urine**: the liquid; neutral or medical usage.
- **urinate**: the formal or medical verb for passing urine.

**faucet** (A10) and **jalapeño** (A22) are already present and were not duplicated. Shower faucet is a contextual use of faucet; shower handle and shower knob are examples under handle and knob. Specialized plumbing terms remain outside this shortlist.
