# Visual preposition coverage audit

This visual overview is deliberately in **EN::Patterns**. The 603 atomic
questions in **EN::Connection Patterns** remain intact; this material
organizes their meaning as one visual map for active production.

The four user-provided reference images were used as a coverage checklist.
Original diagrams, not the internet images, are used in Anki.

## Source-image cautions

- For geographic comparisons, `Canada is north of the United States` is
  natural; `Canada is on the north of America` is not a reliable model.
- To describe moving farther from a starting point, use `away from + place`.
  `Away to` is not the general opposite of `toward`.
- `Between` can refer to three or more distinct identifiable items.
- `Above` and `over`, like `below` and `under`, often overlap.
  Diagrams emphasize common viewpoints, not strict geometric rules.
- `In the front of` can describe an interior front area in a suitable
  context, but `at the front of` or `in the front seat` is often more natural
  in American English; `in front of` usually identifies relative position.
- Spatial `about` can mean roughly `around` (e.g., scattered about the
  room), but this is less common in contemporary American conversation.

## Media delivery

The five SVG assets live under `cards/patterns/media/` and are referenced
from four grouped overview cards. The Patterns builder exports regular
`<img src="...">` references. The Anki desktop add-on puts the exact
versioned filenames into Anki media before updating the notes; the normal
Anki collection/media sync then distributes them.
