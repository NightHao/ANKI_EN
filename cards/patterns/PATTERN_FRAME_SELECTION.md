# Reviewed Production Frames for `EN::Patterns`

This file governs the compact production-frame layer selected from, or checked
against, the larger `EN::Connection Patterns` resource.

Read this file before adding a general production frame to `cards.md`.
Omission and shortening grammar cards are governed separately by
[`OMISSION_GRAMMAR_CARD_SPEC.md`](OMISSION_GRAMMAR_CARD_SPEC.md).

## Purpose

`EN::Connection Patterns` teaches 600 individual connection decisions through
closed retrieval, contrast, and repair prompts. `EN::Patterns` has a different
job: its Front shows a complete reusable frame so that the learner can use that
frame to produce a new sentence.

This is a compact activation layer, not a second copy of the 600-card deck.

## Target capability

After learning this layer, the learner should be able to start with a meaning
they want to express, retrieve a natural contemporary American-English frame,
and complete a new sentence without translating the whole sentence word by
word.

The layer supports the grammatical range, coherence, and paraphrasing needed
for IELTS 7+ performance. It does not by itself guarantee an IELTS band score:
real-time fluency, pronunciation, vocabulary, accuracy, and timed speaking or
writing practice must be trained separately.

Success means that the learner can:

1. state, limit, support, qualify, compare, and conclude a position;
2. explain reasons, results, problems, responses, and degrees of certainty;
3. organize an example, report a source, and describe numerical change;
4. narrate events and changes with clear time relationships;
5. clarify, confirm, and paraphrase when the exact word is unavailable; and
6. choose a frame because its meaning is needed, not because it sounds
   artificially advanced.

## Function coverage matrix

The matrix defines coverage by communicative ability rather than by grammar
chapter. One frame may support more than one row, but every row must have at
least one clear production route.

## Selection rule

Add a frame only when all of these are true:

1. It can be reused in at least five realistic situations.
2. It performs a complete communicative job such as giving a reason,
   presenting evidence, explaining a result, proposing a solution, describing
   a trade-off, setting a condition, or making a comparison.
3. Seeing the full frame materially helps sentence production.
4. It is natural contemporary American English at roughly an IELTS Speaking 7
   level.
5. It is not already owned by an equivalent Front in `cards.md`.

Several validated connection decisions may be consolidated into one production
frame when they share one communicative job and the combined Front remains
usable. Do not merge alternatives whose meaning difference would become
unclear.

## Exclusions

Do not add:

- every validated connection pattern;
- single low-level facts such as `at + clock time` unless they form part of a
  broader production frame;
- recognition-only, spelling-only, or punctuation-only decisions;
- rare or specialist constructions;
- a second card created merely for repetition;
- a frame whose explanation needs a long reference lesson; or
- an omission or shortening rule already owned by the operation-based grammar
  suite.
