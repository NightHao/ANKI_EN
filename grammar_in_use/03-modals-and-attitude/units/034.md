# Unit 34 — `should` 2

## A

Study this recommendation:

> The nutritionist recommended that everyone should eat more beans.

After verbs such as `insist`, `demand`, `recommend`, `suggest`, and `propose`, we can use `that + should + base verb`: *They demanded that the report should be published.* / *What do you suggest we should do?*

We can often omit `should` and use the base form: *The committee recommended that the report be published.* This is the subjunctive. Normal present or past forms are also used in some varieties: *They insisted that he apologised.*

## B

Do not use `to` after `suggest` in this pattern. Say *I suggest that Mira take a taxi* or *What do you suggest we do?* (not *suggest Mira to take*). You can also use `suggest + -ing`: *I suggest taking a taxi.*

## C

After adjectives such as `strange`, `odd`, `natural`, `surprised`, and `important`, `should` can comment on a fact: *It is odd that the lift should stop every Friday.* / *I was surprised that he should refuse.*

## D

`If ... should` presents a less likely possibility: *If the weather should worsen, the match will move indoors.* We can invert the clause: *Should the weather worsen, the match will move indoors.*

## E

`I should` can give advice about another person’s situation: *I should wait until tomorrow* means “I would wait if I were you.”
