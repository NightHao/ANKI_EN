# Unit 19 — Present tenses for the future

## A

Study this conversation:

> “What are you doing on Thursday?”  
> “I’m having dinner with my former manager.”

Use present continuous for a personal future arrangement that is already decided, often with a time: *We’re flying to Seoul next week.* / *When are you meeting the architect?* We do not normally use `will` for this kind of existing arrangement.

The form can also be used just before an action begins, especially with movement verbs: *I’m leaving now.* / *Are you ready? I’m coming.*

## B

Use present simple for a timetable, programme, or other fixed schedule: *The ferry departs at 6:40 tomorrow.* / *What time does the lecture begin?* / *The museum is closed on Monday.*

We can also use present simple for a person’s fixed programme: *My training course starts next Tuesday.* But for an ordinary personal plan, present continuous is more natural: *What time are you seeing the dentist tomorrow?*

## C

Compare the source of the future information:

| Personal arrangement | Timetable |
|---|---|
| *I’m arriving at 7:30.* | *The train arrives at 7:30.* |
| *We’re going to a concert tonight.* | *The concert starts at 8.* |

For an appointment, lesson, or exam, ordinary `have` is also possible: *I have / I’ve got an interview next Wednesday.*

4. We ___ (not / use) the car this evening.
