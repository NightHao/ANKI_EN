# Unit 118 — `like`, `as if`, and `as though`

## A

Study this reaction:

> You look **like** you have not slept. You look **as if** you have not slept.

After *look, sound,* and *feel*, use **like + clause** or **as if/as though + clause** to describe an appearance or impression: *It sounds like the neighbours are moving furniture; I feel as though I need another holiday.* The meaning is an impression, not necessarily a fact.

We can also use a simple adjective after these verbs: *You look tired.* Compare it with *You look as if you have not slept*, which gives a reason for that appearance.

## B

Use the same patterns with other verbs to describe how someone acts:

> He rushed in like he was late for a flight.  
> After the error, she continued as if nothing had happened.  
> They stared at me as though I had said something strange.

`As though` has the same meaning as `as if` and is slightly more formal.

## C

After **as if/as though**, we sometimes use a past form for an unreal present idea:

> He talks as if he knew everyone here. (He does not know everyone.)  
> Why are you treating me as though I were a child?

The past form does not refer to past time. It marks distance from reality. Do not normally use `like` in this unreal pattern.

## D

With `be` in this unreal use, **were** is possible for all persons: *She behaves as if she were the owner.* In informal English, *was* is also common: *as if she was the owner.*

## Exercises

### 118.1

Complete with `like`, `as if`, or `as though`.

1. The sky looks ___ it is going to snow.
2. You sound ___ you have a cold.
3. He spent money ___ it grew on trees.

### 118.2

Choose the meaning.

1. “Mara looks tired.” / “Mara looks as if she has been working all night.” Which sentence gives a reason for the appearance?
2. “They spoke as if they knew the answer.” Is their knowledge presented as real or unreal?

### 118.3

Rewrite using `as if` and an unreal past form.

1. He is not the manager, but he gives orders like a manager.
2. I am not invisible, but they ignore me.
