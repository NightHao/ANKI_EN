# Unit 144 — Phrasal verbs with `up` (2)

## A

Study this exchange:

> “Please do not bring up the budget again.”
> “I only came up with a cheaper idea.”

`Bring up` means introduce a topic in conversation. `Come up` means arise or be mentioned: *A useful question came up in the workshop.* `Come up with` means produce an idea, answer, or solution: *Can you come up with a safer plan?*

## B

`Make something up` means invent something untrue: *He made up an excuse.* `Save up for` means save money for a purpose: *She is saving up for a new laptop.* `Tear something up` means tear it into pieces.

`Break up` or `split up (with somebody)` means end a relationship. `Do up` means fasten clothing or a shoelace; it can also mean repair and improve a building: *They did up the attic.*

## C

`Look something up` means search for information in a dictionary, database, or reference book. `Put up with` means tolerate something unpleasant: *I will not put up with that noise.* Both are separable only in the first form: *look it up*, but *put up with it*.

`Clear up` can mean become bright after bad weather: *It should clear up by evening.* `Cheer up` means become happier; `cheer somebody up` means make them happier.

## D

`Blow up` means explode; `blow something up` means destroy it with an explosion. `Beat somebody up` means hit them repeatedly. `Hold somebody/something up` means delay. `Mix people/things up` or `get them mixed up` means confuse one with another.

## Exercises

### 144.1

Complete the sentences.

1. I do not know this word; I will ___ it ___ .
2. The forecast says the rain should ___ later.
3. They ___ after six years together. (`ended their relationship`)
4. Do not ___ that argument ___ again. (`introduce`)
5. The roadworks ___ us ___ for forty minutes. (`delayed`)

### 144.2

Choose the best meaning or correction.

1. *She made up a story.* (`invented it` / `formed a whole`)
2. *We have to put up with it.* (`tolerate it` / `display it`)
3. Correct: *Look up it online.*
4. *The siblings are often mixed up.* What happens?
