# Unit 139 — Phrasal verbs with `out`

## A

Study the power cut:

> All the lights **went out**, so we **put out** the candles and left.

`Go out` means stop burning or shining. We **put/turn/blow out** a fire, light, or candle. These verbs are separable: *turn out the light / turn the light out / turn it out*.

## B

**Work out** has several uses: exercise (*I work out twice a week*), develop successfully (*I hope it works out*), calculate (*The price works out at $15 each*), and understand/calculate a problem (*Can you work it out?*). **Figure out** is another way to say understand or solve.

## C

Use **carry out** an order, survey, plan, or investigation; **point out** something to draw attention to it; **sort out** a problem or a disordered set of things; and **find out** information.

**Turn out** means become or prove to be: *The trip turned out well; it turned out that the warning was false.* **Give/hand out** means distribute one to each person. **Run out of** means use all of something; **try out** means test a product, system, or idea.
