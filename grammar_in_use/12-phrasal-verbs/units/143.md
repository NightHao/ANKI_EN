# Unit 143 — Phrasal verbs with `up` (1)

## A

Study this story:

> A woman came up to me after the talk and asked how I had set up my website.

`Come/go/walk up to` means approach somebody or something. `Set up` means start an organisation, business, system, or website: *They set up a local repair service.* `Grow up` means become an adult, and `bring up a child` means raise a child.

## B

`Clean up`, `clear up`, and `tidy up` mean make a place clean or orderly. `Wash up` means wash dishes after a meal. `Take up` can mean begin a hobby or activity (*take up climbing*) or use time or space (*The sofa takes up half the room*).

## C

`Turn up` and `show up` mean arrive: *Only six people showed up.* `Use something up` means use all of it: *We used up the last of the paint.* `Fix up` means arrange: *Can we fix up a call for Thursday?*

`Catch up with somebody` means reach a person ahead of you; `catch somebody up` is also possible. `End up` describes the final result: *We missed the train and ended up taking a taxi.*

## D

`Make up` can mean form a whole: *Online orders make up most of our sales.* `Be made up of` means consist of: *The course is made up of four modules.*

`Keep up (with somebody)` means remain at the same speed or level: *I cannot keep up with the reading.* `Keep it up!` encourages someone to continue doing well. `Give up` means stop trying, or stop an activity: *Do not give up.* *He gave up smoking.*

## Exercises

### 143.1

Complete with the correct phrasal verb.

1. We should ___ a meeting before Friday. (`arrange`)
2. My grandparents ___ in a small coastal town. (`became adults`)
3. I bought extra vegetables so they would not go bad, then ___ them ___ in soup. (`used all`)
4. The delayed guests finally ___ at midnight. (`arrived`)
5. The class is ___ ___ students from twelve countries. (`consists of`)

### 143.2

Choose the best completion.

1. You walk ahead; I will (`catch up with you` / `catch you up with`).
2. The piano (`takes up` / `takes`) too much space.
3. After several wrong turns, we (`ended up` / `gave up`) at the ferry terminal.
4. Correct: *Use up it before it expires.*
