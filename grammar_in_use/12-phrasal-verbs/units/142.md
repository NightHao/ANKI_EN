# Unit 142 — Phrasal verbs with `up` and `down`

## A

Study this scene:

> Niko picked up the receipt from the floor, stood up, and put it up on the noticeboard.

`Up` often suggests movement to a higher position: *stand up*, *pick something up*, and *put something up*. With a pronoun object, place it between the verb and particle: *pick it up* (not *pick up it*).

`Down` gives the opposite direction: *sit down*, *bend down*, *put something down*, and *take something down from the wall*. *Lie down* means put your body in a horizontal position.

## B

`Turn something up` means increase volume, heat, or strength: *Turn the speaker up; I cannot hear the podcast.* `Turn something down` means reduce it: *Turn the heating down before you leave.* Both are separable: *turn it up/down*.

## C

Several `down` verbs describe reduction or an ending. `Slow down` means go less fast; `calm down` means become calmer, and `calm somebody down` means make them calmer. `Cut down on` means reduce consumption or frequency: *I am cutting down on sugary drinks.*

`Close down` and `shut down` mean stop operating as a business: *The factory shut down last winter.* `Burn down` means be destroyed by fire: *The warehouse burnt down overnight.*

## D

Other common meanings are not literal direction. `Let somebody down` means disappoint them. `Write something down` means record it for later. `Break down` means stop working for a machine, or fail for a relationship: *The lift broke down.* *Their partnership broke down.*

`Knock/cut/blow something down` means destroy it; `be knocked down` can mean hit by a vehicle. `Turn somebody/something down` means refuse an offer or application: *They turned her down for the role.*

## Exercises

### 142.1

Complete with a phrasal verb from this unit.

1. Please ___ the music ___; the baby is asleep.
2. I dropped my card, so I bent down and ___ it ___ .
3. The old cinema ___ after losing customers.
4. Write the reference number ___ before you forget it.
5. Do not ___ me ___; you promised to help.

### 142.2

Choose the best meaning.

1. *The bus slowed down near the school.* (`became quieter` / `went less fast`)
2. *Her application was turned down.* (`refused` / `made quieter`)
3. *A storm blew the fence down.* (`destroyed it` / `lifted it`)
4. Correct: *Pick up it from the floor.*
