# Unit 141 — Phrasal verbs with `on` and `off`: continuing and reacting

## A

Study the long meeting:

> The discussion **went on** for hours, and one speaker **kept on** repeating the same point.

Use **go on** for an event continuing. **Keep on doing** means continue or repeat an action, often annoyingly. **Drive/walk/play on** mean continue moving or playing. **Go on/carry on + `-ing`** mean continue an activity; **go on/carry on with + noun** mean continue that thing.

## B

Use **doze/drop/nod off** for falling asleep, **go off** for an alarm sounding, **show off** for trying to impress, and **tell somebody off** for speaking angrily because they did something wrong.

## C

Use **finish something off** to complete the last part. **Rip somebody off** means cheat them by charging too much. **Put somebody off (doing) something** means make them not want it or not want to do it.

## D

**Get on** means make progress: *How are you getting on with the report?* **Get on (with somebody)** means have a good relationship. **Get on with something** means return to a necessary task after a pause.

Complete with a suitable phrasal verb.

### 141.2

Choose the meaning.

1. “She is showing off.” (trying to impress / falling asleep)
2. “They do not get on.” (do not like each other / are not progressing)
3. “The smell put me off eating.” (delayed / removed the desire)
