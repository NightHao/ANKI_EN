# Unit 6 — Past continuous

## A

Study this situation:

> At 7:30, Noor was making dinner. Her guests arrived at 8:00.

At 7:30, the cooking had started but was not finished. We use **was/were + verb-ing** for an action in progress at a particular past time: “This time last year, I was living in Lisbon.” Ask “What were you doing at ten last night?”

## B

Compare an activity in progress with a complete past event:

| action in the middle | complete action |
|---|---|
| “We were walking home when it began to snow.” | “We walked home after the party.” |
| “Leo was watching a film when I called.” | “Leo watched films every day while he was ill.” |

We often use past simple for something that happened during the longer activity: “My phone rang while I was paying.” “It was raining when I woke up.” The past continuous sets the scene; past simple moves the story forward.

## C

For a sequence of completed actions, use past simple: “I saw Maya, stopped, and spoke to her.” Compare “When Maya arrived, we were eating” (the meal was already in progress) with “When Maya arrived, we ate” (the meal followed her arrival).

State verbs are not normally continuous. We say “We knew the answer” and “I wanted to leave” (not “were knowing” or “was wanting”).

## Exercises

### 6.1

Complete the verbs.

1. At midnight, I ___ (still / work).
2. I ___ (walk) home when I ___ (hear) a shout.
3. When the guests arrived, we ___ (have) dinner.

### 6.2

Choose the natural sentence.

1. a) When I arrived, they watched TV. b) When I arrived, they were watching TV.
2. a) I was wanting a holiday. b) I wanted a holiday.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**6.1** 1. was still working 2. was walking; heard 3. were having.

**6.2** 1. b: the viewing was already in progress. 2. b: *want* is normally a state verb.
