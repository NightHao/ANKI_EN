# Unit 16 — Past perfect continuous

## A

Study this scene:

> At dawn, the pavement was shiny, although no rain was falling. It had been raining overnight.

`had been + verb-ing` is the past perfect continuous. It describes an activity that was happening before a past moment, often to explain evidence or a result: *Omar’s clothes were dusty because he had been clearing the attic.* / *They were exhausted because they had been travelling all night.*

## B

It can also show duration before another past event: *We had been discussing the plan for an hour when the manager arrived.* / *She had been living in the town only a month when she found a new job.* The activity may stop at that later event or continue beyond it; the form makes the earlier stretch important.

## C

Compare the time viewpoint:

| Before now | Before a past point |
|---|---|
| *I’ve been waiting for twenty minutes. Where is the bus?* | *The bus finally came. I’d been waiting for twenty minutes.* |
| *Kai is breathless; he has been running.* | *Kai was breathless; he had been running.* |

Use present perfect continuous for a situation measured to now, and past perfect continuous when the story is already in the past.

## D

Choose past continuous for what was happening at the particular past moment: *The band was playing when the lights failed.* Use past perfect continuous for an earlier activity: *The band had been playing for ten minutes when the lights failed.*

State verbs normally stay simple: *We had known each other for years* and *She had always had curly hair* (not *had been knowing/having*).

## Exercises

### 16.1

Complete with past perfect continuous.

1. My legs hurt because I ___ (climb) stairs all day.
2. The queue was long; people ___ (wait) since sunrise.
3. The garden was wet because it ___ (rain).

### 16.2

Choose past continuous or past perfect continuous.

1. When I entered, they **(argued / had been arguing)** for nearly an hour.
2. At 8 p.m., they **(were arguing / had been arguing)** in the kitchen.
3. We **(were driving / had been driving)** for four hours when we stopped for fuel.

### 16.3

Correct the sentence where necessary.

1. I was tired because I had been working since dawn.
2. We had been knowing the answer before the quiz began.
3. It was not snowing when we left, but it had been snowing earlier.
