# Unit 3 — Present continuous and present simple 1

## A

Compare the two forms:

| present continuous | present simple |
|---|---|
| an unfinished action happening around now | things in general or repeated actions |
| “The kettle is boiling.” | “Water boils at 100°C.” |
| “What are they discussing?” | “What do you usually discuss?” |
| “I’m getting tired.” | “I always get tired after lunch.” |

We use **am/is/are + -ing** when we see an action or change as in progress. We use the
present simple for a fact, habit or regular pattern.

## B

The continuous is also used for a temporary situation; the simple is used for a
long-term or permanent situation:

- “I’m staying with my aunt until my flat is ready.”
- “My aunt lives near the university.”
- “Kai is working nights this month.”
- “Kai works for an engineering company.”

Temporary does not mean that the action must be happening at the moment you speak.
Kai can say “I’m working nights this month” while he is eating breakfast.

## C

Compare **I always do** and **I’m always doing**:

- “I always leave home at seven.”  
  (= this happens every time; it is a neutral routine)
- “I’m always leaving my keys at work.”  
  (= this happens too often, and I find it annoying)

Continuous **always** commonly expresses irritation or surprise:

- “You’re always checking your phone while I’m talking.”
- “That printer is always breaking down.”

Do not use it when you simply mean *every time*: “I always take the bus to work”
(not normally “I’m always taking the bus to work”).

## Exercises

### 3.1

Choose the more natural form.

1. I usually **walk / am walking**, but this week I **drive / am driving**.
2. Listen. Someone **plays / is playing** the piano upstairs.
3. My parents **live / are living** in Osaka; their home is there.
4. You **always interrupt / are always interrupting** me! Please let me finish.

### 3.2

Complete with a present simple or present continuous form.

1. The river normally ___ slowly, but it ___ very fast today. (flow)
2. “What ___ you ___?” “I’m an architect.” (do)
3. We ___ with friends while our kitchen is repaired. (stay)

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**3.1** 1. walk; am driving 2. is playing 3. live 4. are always interrupting.

**3.2** 1. flows; is flowing 2. do, do 3. are staying.
