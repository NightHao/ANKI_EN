# Unit 5 — Past simple

## A

Study this situation:

> The meeting started at nine, lasted forty minutes and ended before ten.

We use the past simple for completed past actions and states. Regular verbs normally
end in **-ed**:

- “I work in Taipei now. Before that, I worked in Hsinchu.”
- “They invited us, but we decided not to go.”
- “The driver stopped beside the entrance.”

Many common verbs have irregular past forms: **write → wrote, see → saw, go → went,
take → took, buy → bought**. Some do not change: **cut → cut, shut → shut**.

## B

Use **did + subject + base form** in questions, and **didn’t + base form** in
negatives:

- “Did you enjoy the film?”
- “Where did they stay?”
- “I didn’t buy anything.”
- “Did Mei call?” “No, she didn’t.”

The verb after **did/didn’t** is not a past form: “Did he go?” (not “Did he went?”).
When **do** is the main verb, we say “What did you do?” and “I didn’t do much.”

## C

The past of **be** is **was/were**. It does not use **did**. Use **was/wasn’t** with
**I, he, she** and **it**, and **were/weren’t** with **we, you** and **they**. In a
question, move the verb before the subject: “Was she ...?”; “Were they ...?”

- “I was annoyed because the buses were late.”
- “Were you tired after the journey?”
- “The shops weren’t open.”

Compare “Did you go out?” with “Were you at home?” The first question has an ordinary
verb; the second has **be**.

## Exercises

### 5.1

Complete the sentences with the past simple.

1. We ___ (leave) after dinner.
2. I ___ not ___ (see) the message.
3. Where ___ you ___ (buy) that lamp?
4. The door was open, so I ___ (shut) it.

### 5.2

Choose **did, was** or **were**, and add any other necessary word.

1. ___ the weather good during your holiday?
2. What ___ you ___ last weekend?
3. Why ___ the children late?
4. ___ Hana enjoy the concert?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**5.1** 1. left 2. did, see 3. did, buy 4. shut.

**5.2** 1. Was 2. did, do 3. were 4. Did.
