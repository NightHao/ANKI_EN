# Unit 1 — Present continuous

## A

Study this situation:

> Nina is on a video call. She says, “Can I phone you later? I’m cooking dinner.”

The cooking is happening now and it is not finished. **Am/is/are + verb-ing** is the
present continuous:

| subject | form |
|---|---|
| I | am cooking |
| he/she/it | is cooking |
| we/you/they | are cooking |

We use this form for an action in progress at the time of speaking:

- “Please be quiet. I’m listening to the news.”
- “Where’s Leo?” “He’s talking to a customer.”
- “Are you using this chair?”

The auxiliary is necessary: we say “She is waiting” (not “She waiting”).

## B

The action can be in progress around now without happening at this exact moment:

> “I’m reading a book about sleep.”  
> (= I have started it, but I have not finished it.)

We also use the present continuous with periods around now:

- “I’m working longer hours this week.”
- “My parents are staying with us for a few days.”
- “The company isn’t selling much this year.”

## C

We use the present continuous for a change that has already started. Common verbs in
this pattern include **get, become, change, improve, increase, rise, fall, grow, start**
and **begin**:

- “Your pronunciation is getting clearer.”
- “Food prices are rising again.”
- “I’m beginning to understand the system.”

Compare “Prices are rising” with “Prices rise every summer.” The first describes a
change in progress; the second describes a regular pattern.

## Exercises

### 1.1

Complete the sentences with the present continuous.

1. Please call back later. We ___ (have) dinner.
2. Why ___ the baby ___ (cry)?
3. Mia has started a new course. She ___ (learn) Korean.
4. The days ___ (get) shorter.

### 1.2

Correct the verb where necessary.

1. I write an important email right now.
2. They are building a new sports centre near my house.
3. Is your English improve?
4. We’re not using the meeting room today.
