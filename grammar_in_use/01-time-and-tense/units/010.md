# Unit 10 — Present perfect continuous versus simple

## A

Study these two comments after a decorating session:

> “My arms ache. I’ve been painting the hallway.”  
> “Good news: I’ve painted the hallway. You can see the new colour.”

The continuous draws attention to the activity and its effects; it does not say whether it is complete. The simple (`have/has + past participle`) focuses on a completed action or present result. Compare *Someone has been eating cherries* (there are stains and stones) with *Someone has eaten all the cherries* (none remain).

## B

Use the continuous to ask or say **how long** an activity has lasted: *How long have you been searching for a flat?* / *I’ve been searching since May.* It suits ongoing work: *Mina has been answering calls all morning.*

Use the simple for **how much, how many, or how many times**: *How many calls has Mina answered?* / *She has answered twenty.* *I’ve visited the dentist twice this year.* The result or total is the point.

## C

Some verbs normally do not take a continuous form. With states, use present perfect simple even for duration: *I’ve known the route for years.* / *How long have you had that laptop?* (not *have been knowing/having*).

However, `want` and `mean` can describe an unfinished intention in the continuous: *I’ve been meaning to send you the link, but I keep forgetting.*

## Exercises

### 10.1

Choose the better form.

1. The kitchen smells of garlic. Sam **(has cooked / has been cooking)**.
2. The meal is ready. Sam **(has cooked / has been cooking)** dinner.
3. How many kilometres **(have you cycled / have you been cycling)** this week?
4. How long **(have you cycled / have you been cycling)** today?

### 10.2

Complete the pairs with the correct form of the verb.

1. Priya ___ (design) websites since college. She ___ (design) more than forty websites.
2. I ___ (read) your article, but I have not finished it. I ___ (read) twelve pages so far.

### 10.3

Correct the form if necessary.

1. How long have you been knowing your neighbour?
2. I have been meaning to reply, but I have been busy.
3. We have been owning this apartment since 2018.
