# Unit 8 — Present perfect: experience and unfinished time

## A

Study this conversation:

> “Can I recommend the mushroom soup?”  
> “Maybe. I’ve never tasted it.”

Use the present perfect (`have/has + past participle`) for experience at any time up to now. The exact past time is not important: *Have you ever flown in a small plane?* / *No, I haven’t.*  
`ever` often makes a question about experience, and `never` means “not at any time until now”: *That is the funniest podcast I’ve ever heard.*

Use `been to` for a visit: *I’ve been to Mexico twice.* It means that I went there and returned; it is not the same as *I’ve gone to Mexico*.

## B

The period can also be a recent one that still includes now:

> It is 4 p.m. on Tuesday. “I’ve answered twelve messages today.”

Today has not finished, so present perfect is natural. Similarly, say *Have you spoken to Noor this week?*, *We’ve made good progress this month*, and *I haven’t had any coffee this morning* (if the morning is still continuing for the speaker).

Other continuing time expressions include `recently`, `so far`, `in the last few days`, and `since I arrived`: *So far, nobody has complained.* / *It has snowed twice since we got here.*

## C

Use present perfect after `This/It is the first, second, third ... time`:

> “The lift has stopped again.”  
> “This is the third time it has done that today.”

The occasion is now, but each earlier occurrence belongs to the time leading to now. Say *It’s the first time I’ve cooked for twenty people* or *She has never driven at night before.* Do not use present simple in this pattern: *It’s the first time I’ve used this app* (not *I use*).

## Exercises

### 8.1

Complete with the present perfect form of the verb. Add `ever` or `never` where it is shown.

1. ___ you ___ (watch, ever) a live opera?
2. I ___ (read, never) a novel by that author.
3. This is the best dessert we ___ (have, ever).
4. My cousin ___ (be) to Nairobi, but only once.

### 8.2

Choose the better form.

1. It is still Friday morning: I **(have completed / completed)** two tasks today.
2. At the end of last Friday: I **(have completed / completed)** two tasks that day.
3. We have no news; nothing **(has happened / happened)** so far.
4. This is the second time the printer **(has failed / fails)** this week.

### 8.3

Write one question about experience and one sentence about an unfinished period. Use the prompts.
