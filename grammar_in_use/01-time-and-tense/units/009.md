# Unit 9 — Present perfect continuous

## A

Study this situation:

> Leo opens the door with flour on his shirt. “Sorry, I’ve been making bread.”

`have/has been + verb-ing` is the present perfect continuous. We use it for a recent activity that has just stopped, especially when there is evidence or a reason to ask about it: *Why are your shoes muddy? Have you been walking in the rain?* / *I’ve been trying to call you.* The activity may be unfinished.

## B

Use the same form for an activity that began earlier and is still continuing:

> “How long have you been waiting?”  
> “I’ve been waiting since quarter past six.”

It is especially common with `how long`, `for`, and `since`: *The neighbours have been renovating their flat for weeks.* / *She has been practising the violin since January.* A repeated activity can also continue over a period: *We’ve been meeting online every Thursday this term.*

## C

Compare a present situation with its history:

| Now | Activity before now |
|---|---|
| *Please be quiet. I’m recording.* | *I’ve been recording all afternoon, so I need a break.* |
| *It’s raining.* | *It’s been raining; the path is slippery.* |

The present continuous describes what is happening at this moment. The present perfect continuous looks back from now to an activity that continues or has only recently ended. *I’m looking for my keys* means “now”; *I’ve been looking for my keys* explains the recent search.

## Exercises

### 9.1

Make questions with the present perfect continuous.

1. Your friend is breathless after arriving. (you / run?)
2. A colleague started at the company in March and still works there. (how long / you / work / here?)
3. Your child has paint on both hands. (what / you / do?)

### 9.2

Complete the sentences.

1. The alarm started at 7:00 and it is still ringing. It ___ (ring) for ten minutes.
2. I began a Spanish course last autumn and continue now. I ___ (learn) Spanish since last autumn.
3. Our team meets every Monday and began doing this years ago. We ___ (meet) on Mondays for years.

### 9.3

Choose present continuous or present perfect continuous.

1. Please do not interrupt: I ___ (write) a report.
2. I ___ (write) reports all day, so my eyes hurt.
3. Why ___ you ___ (stare) at the screen?
4. The tap ___ (drip) since breakfast.

<summary>Answers</summary>

9.1 1. Have you been running? 2. How long have you been working here? 3. What have you been doing?

9.2 1. has been ringing 2. have been learning 3. have been meeting.

9.3 1. am writing 2. have been writing 3. are / staring 4. has been dripping.
