# Unit 102 — `so` and `such`

## A

Study this exchange:

> “Was the concert good?” “The drummer was **so** energetic.”  
> “Yes, and it was **such a** lively crowd.”

Use **so + adjective/adverb**: *so energetic, so late, so carefully*. Use **such + noun**, or **such + adjective + noun**: *such noise, such helpful neighbours*. Before a singular countable noun, say **such a/an**: *such a useful map* (not *a such useful map*).

## B

`So` and `such` often introduce a result with **that**. The result is usually clear even without *that*.

> The path was so icy (that) we turned back.  
> It was such a noisy café (that) nobody could hear the guide.

They make the description stronger: *I’m so relieved* means “very relieved”; *We had such good luck* means “really good luck”.

## C

We also use them when reacting to the degree of something already known or expected.

> I had no idea the museum was so close.  
> Why would anyone say such a cruel thing?

Here **so + adjective/adverb** points to a degree (*so close*), while **such + noun phrase** points to a type of thing (*such a cruel thing*). **No such + noun** means that the thing does not exist: *There is no such rule in the handbook.*

## D

Some common noun phrases use **such**, not *so*: *such a long wait, such a long distance, such a lot of work*. But use **so much/many + noun**: *so much luggage; so many messages*. We also say *I haven’t called her for so long*.

## Exercises

### 102.1

Complete with `so`, `such`, or `such a`.

1. The instructions were ___ unclear that we asked for help.
2. We met ___ interesting people on the course.
3. It was ___ narrow street that the van could not enter.
4. Why are you walking ___ slowly?

### 102.2

Make one sentence using `so` or `such`.

1. The queue was very long. We missed the start of the film.
2. The apartment has an excellent view. We decided to rent it immediately.

### 102.3

Choose the natural completion.

1. I did not realise the station was (so far / such far).
2. There is (no such word / no so word) in English.
3. We had (so much traffic / such much traffic) on the way home.

**102.1** 1. so  2. such  3. such a  4. so.  
**102.2** 1. The queue was so long that we missed the start of the film. 2. It had such an excellent view that we decided to rent the apartment immediately.  
**102.3** 1. so far  2. no such word  3. so much traffic.
