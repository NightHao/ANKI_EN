# Unit 112 — `even`

## A

Study this comment about a new restaurant:

> The menu pleased everyone — **even** my aunt, who usually dislikes eating out.

Use **even** to highlight something surprising or extreme. Put it before the person, thing, or phrase that is unexpectedly included: *even on Sundays; even with a map; not even the manager knew*. With a verb, it usually goes in the middle: *She even remembered my birthday; They do not even have a website.*

## B

Use **even + comparative** when a difference is greater than an earlier surprising difference:

> The first train was crowded, but the next one was even fuller.  
> I expected a delay; I was even more annoyed when the notice disappeared.

It can also show a smaller amount: *We had little time, and then we had even less.*

## C

Use **even though, even when,** or **even if + subject + verb**. **Even though** describes a real fact: *Even though I was nervous, I spoke.* **Even when** describes a time or repeated situation: *He smiles even when he is exhausted.* **Even if** means that the result will not change under a possible condition: *I will finish it even if it takes all evening.*

Do not use `even + subject + verb` alone for these meanings.

## D

Compare these sentences:

> We will go for a walk even if it rains. (Rain will not change the plan.)  
> We will go for a walk if it does not rain. (Rain will change the plan.)

Without `if`, use **even + noun phrase**: *The hill is difficult even for experienced runners.*

## Exercises

### 112.1

Add `even` in the best position.

1. The guide speaks four languages. (Japanese)
2. I cannot remember the name of the street. (the first letter)
3. The museum is open on public holidays.

### 112.2

Complete with `even` and a comparative.

1. This chair is comfortable, but that one is ___ .
2. The second test was ___ difficult than the first.

### 112.3

Choose `even though`, `even when`, or `even if`.

1. I will take the course ___ it is demanding.
2. ___ the forecast is sunny, bring a jacket for the evening.
3. She checks the door twice ___ she is in a hurry.
