# Unit 108 — Superlatives

## A

Study this question:

> Which is **the quickest** way home? “The subway is **the most reliable** option tonight.”

Use superlatives to identify the highest degree in a group. Many short words take **-est**: *longest, hottest, easiest*. Longer words use **most**: *the most practical, the most enjoyable*. We normally use **the**, or a possessive word: *the busiest week; my best idea*.

## B

Some forms are irregular: *good → best, bad → worst, far → furthest/farthest*. The spelling rules follow comparative patterns: *thin → thinnest; happy → happiest*. Compare a superlative with a comparative:

> This is the cheapest shop in the area.  
> This shop is cheaper than the others nearby.

We often say **one of the + superlative + plural noun**: *one of the friendliest cafés in town*.

## C

Use **oldest** for things and general comparisons: *the oldest bridge in the region*. **Eldest** is only for a person within a family: *their eldest child*. **Oldest** is possible for family members too.

## D

Name the group with **in** for a place, organisation, or group of people: *the tallest building in the city; the newest employee in the team*. Use **of** for a period or a set: *the best day of my life; the quieter of the two rooms*.

## E

We often use a present perfect after a superlative when talking about experience:

> That is the funniest podcast I have ever heard.  
> It was the hardest decision I have made this year.

## Exercises

### 108.1

Write the superlative form.

1. busy → ___
2. useful → ___
3. bad → ___
4. far → ___

### 108.2

Complete with `in` or `of`.

1. It is the oldest house ___ the street.
2. Friday was the busiest day ___ the month.
3. She is one of the most experienced engineers ___ our company.

### 108.3

Complete the sentences.

1. This is one of the ___ (quiet) parks in the city.
2. Mei is the ___ child in her family. (`elder`)
3. That was the ___ meal I have ever cooked. (`good`)

<summary>Answers</summary>

**108.1** 1. busiest  2. most useful  3. worst  4. furthest or farthest.  
**108.2** 1. in  2. of  3. in.  
**108.3** 1. quietest  2. eldest  3. best.
