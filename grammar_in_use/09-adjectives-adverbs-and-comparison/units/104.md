# Unit 104 — `quite`, `pretty`, `rather`, and `fairly`

## A

Study these comments after a workshop:

> “The speaker was **quite** interesting.” “Yes, and the questions were **pretty** useful.”

With ordinary adjectives, **quite** and **pretty** usually mean more than a little but less than very: *quite busy; pretty good*. **Pretty** is informal and especially common in conversation. Before a singular countable noun, say **quite a/an + adjective + noun**: *quite a difficult decision*. We can also say *quite a surprise*, *quite a lot of emails*, and *I quite enjoy podcasts*.

## B

**Rather** is close in strength to *quite*, but often suggests an unwelcome quality: *rather damp; rather inconvenient*. With a positive adjective, it often means “surprisingly”: *That was rather generous of her.*

## C

**Fairly** is weaker and more neutral. *The route is fairly easy* means it is reasonably easy, perhaps not completely easy. Compare:

| Comment | Likely feeling |
|---|---|
| The meal was rather disappointing. | stronger criticism or surprise |
| The meal was fairly disappointing. | mild criticism |

## D

**Quite** can also mean **completely** with certain words: *quite certain, quite right, quite impossible, quite different*. It has this meaning with some verbs too: *I quite agree.* **Not quite** means “not completely”: *The design is not quite ready.*

Compare *The proposal is quite good* (moderately good) with *The information is quite correct* (completely correct). The adjective helps us understand which meaning is intended.

## Exercises

### 104.1

Choose `quite`, `pretty`, `rather`, or `fairly`.

1. The café is ___ crowded today, but we can find a table.
2. It is ___ late to start a three-hour hike now.
3. The instructions are ___ simple, though one step needs care.

### 104.2

Put the words in order.

1. surprise / it / quite / a / was
2. enjoy / I / quite / cooking

### 104.3

Does `quite` mean “moderately” or “completely”?

1. I am quite sure the door was locked.
2. Her new office is quite comfortable.
3. Your answer is quite correct.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**104.1** 1. fairly  2. rather  3. pretty. Other choices may work in a suitable context, but these best match the intended tone.  
**104.2** 1. It was quite a surprise. 2. I quite enjoy cooking.  
**104.3** 1. completely  2. moderately  3. completely.
