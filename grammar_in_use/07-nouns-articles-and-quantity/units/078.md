# Unit 78 — Names with and without `the`: buildings and organisations

## A

Study this message:

> Meet me outside Victoria Station, then we can walk through Green Park to Buckingham Palace.

Most named streets, squares, parks, airports, stations, universities, castles, cathedrals, and palaces have no `the`: *Park Lane, Central Square, Green Park, Dublin Airport, Oxford University, Edinburgh Castle*.

The first word is usually a proper name. Compare *Buckingham Palace* with *the Royal Palace*: `Royal` is an adjective, not a name.

## B

Many other building names take `the`: *the Imperial Hotel, the Grand Theatre, the National Museum, the White House, the Eiffel Tower*. We often omit the final noun: *the Imperial*, *the National*.

Names containing `of` normally take `the`: *the Museum of Science*, *the Tower of London*, *the University of Edinburgh*. Compare *Edinburgh University* (no `the`).

## C

Shops, cafés, and banks named after people usually have no `the`: *Harris’s, Patel’s, Barclays*. Churches named after saints also have no `the`: *St Mark’s Church*.

## D

Most newspapers and many organisations use `the`: *the Guardian, the World Health Organization, the Red Cross*. Most company and airline names do not: *Samsung, Lufthansa, Kenya Airways*.

## Exercises

### 78.1

Add `the` where necessary.

1. We visited ___ National Gallery after lunch.
2. Her train arrives at ___ King’s Cross Station.
3. ___ University of Bristol is near the city centre.
4. He works for ___ British Airways.
5. The article appeared in ___ Times.

### 78.2

Choose the correct form.

1. (`the Royal Hotel` / `Royal Hotel`)
2. (`the McDonald’s` / `McDonald’s`)
3. (`the St Paul’s Cathedral` / `St Paul’s Cathedral`)
4. (`the Museum of Modern Art` / `Museum of Modern Art`)

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**78.1** 1. *the* 2. No article 3. *The* 4. No article 5. *the*.

**78.2** 1. *the Royal Hotel* 2. *McDonald’s* 3. *St Paul’s Cathedral* 4. *the Museum of Modern Art*.
