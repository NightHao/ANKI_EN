# Unit 76 — `the` for types, groups, and nationalities

## A

Study this quiz question:

> “When was the bicycle invented?”

We can use `the + singular noun` to talk about a whole type of animal, invention, or machine: *The whale is a mammal.* *The smartphone has changed communication.* This does not mean one particular whale or phone.

Use `the` with musical instruments when speaking about the skill: *Can you play the violin?* But use `a/an` for one instrument: *There is a violin in the cupboard.* `man` without `the` can mean human beings generally: *The origins of man are widely discussed.*

## B

`The + adjective` can mean a group of people: *the young, the elderly, the rich, the unemployed, the injured*. These expressions are plural: *The injured were taken to hospital.* Do not add `-s`: not *the richs*.

For one person, use a noun: *a wealthy person*, *an unemployed worker*, *a homeless man*.

## C

We say `the French`, `the British`, `the Spanish`, `the Chinese`, and `the Swiss` for people of those nationalities. These usually have plural meaning: *The Spanish are proud of their regional traditions.*

Other nationality nouns usually take a plural `-s` without `the`: *Italians*, *Canadians*, *Mexicans*. You can always say `French people`, `Chinese people`, or `Mexican people`.

## Exercises

### 76.1

Add `the`, `a/an`, or no article.

1. ___ eagle has excellent eyesight.
2. Can you play ___ drums?
3. There is ___ guitar beside the sofa.
4. ___ homeless need safe places to sleep.
5. ___ Brazilians are famous for their love of football. (general statement)

### 76.2

Correct each phrase.

1. The olds need better transport.
2. I met a French at the conference.
3. The Japanese is an island nation. (You mean its people.)
4. I would like to own the piano, but I cannot play a piano.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**76.1** 1. *The* 2. *the* 3. *a* 4. *The* 5. No article.

**76.2** 1. *The old need better transport* / *Old people need better transport.* 2. *I met a French person/woman/man at the conference.* 3. *The Japanese are the people of Japan.* 4. *I would like to own a piano, but I cannot play the piano.*
