# Unit 77 — Names with and without `the`: places

## A

Study this itinerary:

> We will fly from Japan to Canada, then spend three days in Vancouver.

Most names of continents, countries, states, islands, cities, towns, and individual mountains have no `the`: *Asia, Brazil, California, Malta, Nairobi, Mount Fuji*. Names containing `Republic`, `Kingdom`, or `States` normally take `the`: *the United Kingdom*, *the United States*, *the Republic of Korea*.

## B

Titles with a person’s name also have no `the`: *Professor Malik*, *Queen Sofia*, *Doctor Chen*. Compare *I need to see the doctor* (any relevant doctor) with *I need to see Doctor Chen* (a named doctor).

Use `Mount` and `Lake` before a name without `the`: *Mount Kenya*, *Lake Geneva*. But use `the lake` for an unnamed lake in the situation.

## C

Use `the` with oceans, seas, rivers, canals, and deserts: *the Pacific, the Baltic Sea, the Danube, the Panama Canal, the Atacama Desert*.

## D

Use `the` with plural names: *the Martins* (the Martin family), *the Netherlands*, *the Maldives*, *the Rockies*. We also say *the north of the country*, but *northern France*; *the west of Ireland*, but *western Ireland*.

## Exercises

### 77.1

Add `the` where necessary.

1. We crossed ___ Indian Ocean on the way to Australia.
2. ___ Mount Kilimanjaro is in Tanzania.
3. Her family moved to ___ United States last year.
4. ___ River Thames flows through London.
5. They went hiking in ___ Andes.

### 77.2

Correct the name phrase.

1. the Lake Como
2. Doctor the Patel
3. southern of Spain
4. Sahara Desert

<summary>Answers</summary>

**77.1** 1. *the* 2. No article 3. *the* 4. *The* 5. *the*.

**77.2** 1. *Lake Como* 2. *Doctor Patel* 3. *southern Spain* / *the south of Spain* 4. *the Sahara Desert*.
