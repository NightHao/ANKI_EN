# Unit 82 — Reflexive pronouns

## A

Study this introduction:

> “Hello, I’m Ren. Let me introduce myself.”

Use `myself`, `yourself`, `himself`, `herself`, `itself`, `ourselves`, `yourselves`, and `themselves` when the subject and object are the same person or thing: *I cut myself while cooking.* *The children enjoyed themselves at the fair.*

Compare *I introduced her to the team* with *I introduced myself to the team.*

## B

Do not add a reflexive pronoun after `feel`, `relax`, `concentrate`, or `meet`: *I feel better today.* *Try to concentrate.* *Let’s meet at eight.* We also normally say *I got dressed*, *he shaved*, and *she washed* without a reflexive pronoun.

## C

Use `-selves` when every person does something to the same group: *They looked at themselves in the photo.* Use `each other` or `one another` when the action goes between people: *They looked at each other.*

## D

Reflexive pronouns can also emphasise who did something: *I made the table myself.* *You can decide for yourself.* *The guide itself is clear, but the website is confusing.* Here the reflexive pronoun means “not someone/something else”.

## Exercises

### 82.1

Complete with a reflexive pronoun where necessary.

1. Be careful not to burn ___ on the oven door.
2. We had a wonderful time and really enjoyed ___ .
3. I cannot ___ on this noisy train. (`concentrate`)
4. The cat cleaned ___ after eating.
5. Shall we ___ outside the library? (`meet`)

### 82.2

Choose `themselves` or `each other`.

1. The twins often borrow clothes from ___ .
2. The dancers watched ___ in the mirrored wall.
3. We gave ___ small gifts at the end of the course.
4. They painted the kitchen ___ . (Emphasise who did it.)

<summary>Answers</summary>

**82.1** 1. *yourself* 2. *ourselves* 3. No reflexive pronoun: *concentrate* 4. *itself* 5. No reflexive pronoun: *meet*.

**82.2** 1. *each other* 2. *themselves* 3. *each other* 4. *themselves*.
