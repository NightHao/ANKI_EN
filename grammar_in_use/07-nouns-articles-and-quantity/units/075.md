# Unit 75 — General and specific plural reference

## A

Study these two sentences:

> I enjoy documentaries. The documentaries we watched last night were excellent.

Do not use `the` for people or things in general: *Dogs need exercise.* *Teachers often work late.* *I like spicy food.* We also say `most people`, `most cities`, and `most shops` (not *the most people*).

## B

Use `the` for a definite group or thing: *The dogs next door bark at night.* *The food on this plate is too salty.* The listener can identify the group from the phrase or situation.

Compare *All phones need power* (phones in general) with *All the phones on this desk are charged* (a particular set).

## C

A phrase after a noun does not automatically make it specific. *I like books that make me laugh* still means books in general. But *I liked the book you lent me* names one known book.

Compare *I like dark chocolate* (a general preference) with *The dark chocolate you brought was delicious* (a particular chocolate).

## Exercises

### 75.1

Add `the` where necessary.

1. ___ Cats are usually independent animals.
2. Could you feed ___ cats in the kitchen?
3. I do not like ___ traffic in large cities.
4. ___ traffic outside our hotel kept us awake.
5. Most ___ people enjoy a day off.

### 75.2

Choose the correct sentence.

1. (`I love the music.` / `I love music.`) You mean music in general.
2. (`The water in this bottle is warm.` / `Water in this bottle is warm.`)
3. (`All bicycles have wheels.` / `All the bicycles have wheels.`) You mean bicycles in general.
4. Correct: *I like the people who are honest.* (You mean honest people in general.)
