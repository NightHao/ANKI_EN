# Unit 74 — `school` / `the school` and similar places

## A

Study this situation:

> Leo is at school learning French. His father is at the school to collect a report.

For `school`, `university`, `college`, `hospital`, `prison`, and `church`, no article often means the usual purpose of the place. A child *goes to school* as a student; a patient is *in hospital*; somebody *goes to church* for a service.

## B

Use `the` when you mean a particular building, not its normal purpose: *The plumber went to the school to repair a pipe.* *I went to the hospital to visit my uncle.* *Tourists were taking photographs outside the church.*

Compare *Mina is at university* (she studies there) with *I met Mina at the university* (a location). Most other places normally need `the`: *the station*, *the museum*, *the supermarket*.

## C

We say `go to bed`, `be in bed`, `go to work`, `be at work`, and `go home` without `the`: *I finish work at six.* *Let’s go home.*

Use `the` for the physical object: *The cat is under the bed.* Use `at home` for location: *She works at home on Fridays.*

## Exercises

### 74.1

Choose `school` or `the school`.

1. The children are still at ___ .
2. I went to ___ to speak to the head teacher.
3. What time does ___ finish in your country?
4. The roof of ___ needs repairs.

Add `the` where necessary.

1. My cousin is in hospital after an operation.
