# Unit 80 — Noun + noun combinations

## A

Study this notice:

> The train station manager asked passengers to keep their ticket numbers ready.

Two nouns can form one idea. The first noun describes the second: *a train station* is a station for trains; *a ticket number* is the number on a ticket; *a language course* is a course about a language.

The first word can be an `-ing` form: *a washing machine*, *a swimming pool*, *a parking ticket*. Longer chains are possible: *the hotel reception desk*, *a road safety campaign*.

## B

Some combinations are one word and some are two: *toothpaste*, *headache*, *car park*, *road sign*. There is no dependable spelling rule; when unsure, two words are usually safer.

## C

Compare a noun+noun combination with `of`:

| Type or purpose | Contents |
|---|---|
| *a coffee cup* (a cup for coffee) | *a cup of coffee* (coffee in it) |
| *a shopping bag* (a bag for shopping) | *a bag of shopping* (shopping inside) |

## D

The first noun is normally singular even when the meaning is plural: *a car park*, *an apple tree*, *a shoe shop*. Use a plural only when it is part of a fixed expression, such as *sports car*.

## E

Measurements before a noun are singular and hyphenated: *a two-week course*, *a thirty-minute meeting*, *a six-year-old child*. After the verb, use the normal plural: *The course lasts two weeks.*

## Exercises

### 80.1

Make noun+noun combinations.

1. a machine for washing dishes
2. a shop that sells bicycles
3. problems connected with traffic
4. a desk at a hotel where guests check in

### 80.2

Choose the correct form.

1. We booked a (`three-night` / `three-nights`) stay.
2. The stay lasted three (`night` / `nights`).
3. I need a (`coffee cup` / `cup of coffee`) because I want something to drink now.
4. This is a (`five-year-old` / `five-years-old`) building.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**80.1** 1. *a dishwashing machine* 2. *a bicycle shop* 3. *traffic problems* 4. *a hotel reception desk*.

**80.2** 1. *three-night* 2. *nights* 3. *cup of coffee* 4. *five-year-old*.
