# Unit 87 — `much`, `many`, `little`, `few`, and quantity phrases

## A

Study this comment:

> We have plenty of chairs, but very little space.

Use `much` and `little` with uncountable nouns: *much time*, *little patience*. Use `many` and `few` with plural countable nouns: *many visitors*, *few buses*. `A lot of`, `lots of`, and `plenty of` work with both: *lots of work / lots of emails*.

`Plenty of` means more than enough: *There is plenty of food for everyone.*

## B

In positive statements, `much` is often unnatural, especially in conversation. Say *We spent a lot of money*, not usually *We spent much money*. But `much` is normal in negatives and questions: *I do not have much energy.* *Did you sleep much?*

It also follows `too`, `so`, and `as`: *That is too much noise.* *I do not earn as much as he does.* `Many` and `a lot of` are both normal with plural countables, though `many years` is especially common.

## C

`Little` and `few` mean “not much/not many”, often less than wanted: *We have little hope of catching the train.* *Few people knew the answer.* Add `very` to strengthen this: *very little rain*, *very few errors*.

## D

`A little` means some amount; `a few` means some number: *I have a little time before class.* *I have a few questions.* They suggest enough for the present purpose.

Compare *She has few friends* (not many) with *She has a few friends* (some friends). We say `only a little` and `only a few`, not *only little/few*.

## Exercises

### 87.1

Choose the correct phrase.

1. There are (`much` / `many`) reasons to celebrate.
2. I do not have (`many` / `much`) luggage.
3. We have (`plenty of` / `much`) time, so do not rush.
4. The town gets (`few` / `a few`) tourists in winter; it is almost empty.
5. Could you give me (`little` / `a little`) advice?

### 87.2

Correct or complete the sentence.

1. She drinks much coffee every morning.
2. We have only ___ minutes before the doors close. (`a few`)
3. I made ___ mistakes, so I passed easily. (`few`)
4. This bag is ___ heavy for me to lift. (`too`)

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**87.1** 1. *many* 2. *much* 3. *plenty of* 4. *few* 5. *a little*.

**87.2** 1. *She drinks a lot of coffee every morning.* 2. *a few* 3. *few* 4. *too*.
