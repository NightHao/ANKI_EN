# Unit 81 — Possessive `'s` and `of`

## A

Study this message:

> I found my neighbour’s cat in the garden. Its collar had the vet’s phone number on it.

We usually use `'s` for people and animals: *my colleague’s laptop*, *the dog’s bowl*, *Nora’s parents*. You can omit the repeated noun: *That seat is my brother’s.*

It can also describe the intended user or source: *a baby’s spoon* is a spoon for a baby, and *a bird’s nest* is a nest made by a bird.

Do not attach `'s` to a long phrase. Say *the address of the woman we met yesterday*, not *the woman we met yesterday’s address*.

## B

With singular nouns use `'s`: *the child’s coat*. With regular plurals ending in `-s`, use only an apostrophe: *the students’ projects*. With irregular plurals, use `'s`: *the children’s playground*, *the men’s changing room*.

For a shared possession, put `'s` on the final name: *Liam and Zoe’s apartment*. If they own separate things, use both possessives: *Liam’s and Zoe’s bicycles*.

## C

For things, ideas, and longer noun groups, `of` is often more natural: *the cover of the book*, *the temperature of the room*, *the beginning of the film*. We commonly say `the top/bottom/front/back/side of` something.

For organisations and places, either form is often possible: *the company’s decision / the decision of the company*; *the city’s parks / the parks of the city*.

## D

Use `'s` with time expressions: *today’s meeting*, *last night’s storm*, *a day’s work*, *two weeks’ notice*, *fifteen minutes’ walk*.

## Exercises

### 81.1

Choose the correct form.

1. My (`sister’s` / `sisters’`) room is upstairs. (One sister.)
2. The (`children’s` / `childrens’`) toys are everywhere.
3. We borrowed (`Rita and Omar’s` / `Rita’s and Omar’s`) car. (They share one car.)
4. The end (`of the road` / `road’s`) is closed.

### 81.2

Rewrite using a possessive or `of` phrase.

1. the meeting next Thursday
2. a holiday lasting four weeks
3. the bicycle belonging to the man in the green coat
4. the policy of the organisation

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**81.1** 1. *sister’s* 2. *children’s* 3. *Rita and Omar’s* 4. *of the road*.

**81.2** 1. *next Thursday’s meeting* 2. *four weeks’ holiday* 3. *the bicycle of the man in the green coat* 4. *the organisation’s policy* / *the policy of the organisation*.
