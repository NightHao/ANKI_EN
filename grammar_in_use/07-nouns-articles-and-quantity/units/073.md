# Unit 73 — `the`: unique things and common reference

## A

Study this question:

> “What is the quickest route to the station?”

Use `the` for something unique in the relevant context: *the main entrance*, *the end of the road*, *the capital of Kenya*. We also use it with superlatives: *the highest mountain*, *the most useful app*, and with `the same`: *These two files have the same name.*

## B

Some things are unique in the world or our surroundings: *the sun, the moon, the earth, the sky, the sea, the world*. But use `Earth` without `the` when treating it as a planet: *Earth is smaller than Jupiter.* Use `space` without `the` for the universe: *Satellites travel through space.*

## C

We say `the cinema`, `the theatre`, `the radio`, and `the internet` for these activities or systems. But say `watch TV` and `on television`; use `the television` for a particular TV set: *Could you move the television?*

## D

Meals normally have no article: *We had lunch at noon.* Add `a/an` when describing the meal: *They served a memorable dinner.* Labels use no article: *platform 6*, *room 204*, *page 11*, *vitamin D*.

## Exercises

### 73.1

Add `the`, `a/an`, or no article.

1. ___ sun is ___ nearest star to Earth.
2. We ate ___ breakfast, then walked to ___ sea.
3. Our flight leaves from ___ gate 12.
4. I listen to ___ radio in the car, but I do not watch ___ TV much.

### 73.2

Correct the article use where necessary.

1. Mars is nearest planet to the Earth.
2. The lunch was excellent.
3. Can you turn off television? (You mean the set in this room.)
4. The space is vast and mostly empty. (You mean the universe.)

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**73.1** 1. *The; the* 2. No article; *the* 3. No article 4. *the;* no article.

**73.2** 1. *Mars is the nearest planet to Earth.* 2. Correct if a particular lunch is meant. 3. *Can you turn off the television?* 4. *Space is vast and mostly empty.*
