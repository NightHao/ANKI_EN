# Unit 72 — `a/an` and `the`

## A

Study this story:

> I found a key outside the gate. I gave the key to the building manager.

Use `a/an` when you mention one thing for the first time and it is not yet identified. Use `the` when the listener can identify it from what has already been said. *A cyclist stopped beside us. The cyclist asked for directions.*

## B

Use `the` for a particular person or thing; use `a/an` for one of a kind. *I sat on a chair* means any suitable chair. *I sat on the chair by the window* identifies one chair.

Compare *She works at a hotel* (a type of workplace) with *The hotel she works at is near the harbour* (a known place).

## C

Use `the` when the situation makes the reference obvious: *Please close the window* (the window in this room). *I took a taxi to the airport.* We often say `the bank`, `the post office`, `the doctor`, and `the dentist` when a particular local service is meant.

Compare: *Is there a pharmacy near here?* (any pharmacy) and *I need to go to the pharmacy* (the one the speaker has in mind). A job title uses `a/an`: *My brother is a dentist.*

## D

Use `a/an` in expressions of rate and frequency: *twice a month*, *sixty kilometres an hour*, *four hours a day*. Here `a` means “per”.

## Exercises

### 72.1

Add `a/an` or `the`.

1. I bought ___ map. ___ map shows the walking trails.
2. Could you switch off ___ fan, please?
3. We stayed in ___ small guesthouse. ___ guesthouse had a roof terrace.
4. Is there ___ cash machine near this station?

### 72.2

Choose the correct completion.

1. I go to (`a dentist` / `the dentist`) twice ___ year.
2. My aunt is (`a doctor` / `the doctor`) at the local clinic.
3. The train costs €18 ___ ticket. (`a` / `the`)
4. We met at (`a café` / `the café`) opposite your office—the one you recommended.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**72.1** 1. *a; The* 2. *the* 3. *a; The* 4. *a*.

**72.2** 1. *the dentist; a* 2. *a doctor* 3. *a* 4. *the café*.
