# Unit 49 — Question formation

## A

Study this exchange at a hotel desk:

> “The museum closes at five.”  
> “What time **does** it open tomorrow?”

In most questions, the subject follows the first verb in the verb phrase:

> **Are** the cafés open?  
> **Have** you booked a table?  
> **Was** the bridge damaged?

With present simple and past simple main verbs, we add `do`, `does`, or `did`, then use the base verb:

> Where **do** you work?  
> When **did** the train leave?  
> What time **does** the shop close?

We do not say `What time does the shop closes?`

## B

Question words can be either the subject or the object. This decides whether we need `do/does/did`.

> **Who called** you? *(who = the caller)*  
> **Who did you call**? *(you = the caller; who = object)*

> **What happened** after lunch?  
> **What did you buy** after lunch?

When `who`, `what`, `which`, or `how many` is the subject, do not add `do`:

> Which bus goes to the airport?  
> How many people came?

## C

Prepositions usually stay at the end in ordinary conversation:

> Where are you from?  
> Who are you waiting for?  
> Which role did she apply for?

`To whom did you speak?` is possible, but it is formal and unusual in relaxed speech.

Negative questions often show surprise or invite agreement:

> Didn’t you see my message?  
> Haven’t we met somewhere before?  
> Why don’t we eat outside tonight?

Answer the meaning, not the negative word: “Don’t you want tea?” — “Yes, I do.”

## Exercises

### 49.1

Make questions.

1. `your sister / live / nearby?`  
2. `why / the flight / late?`  
3. `who / send / this photo?`

### 49.2

Choose the correct question.

1. `What happened?` / `What did happen?` (You want to know about an accident.)  
2. `Who did Mia invite?` / `Who invited Mia?` (Mia invited a person.)

### 49.3

You expected your friend to know the answer. Make a surprised negative question: `you / not / hear / the announcement?`

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**49.1** 1. Does your sister live nearby? 2. Why was the flight late? 3. Who sent this photo?

**49.2** 1. `What happened?` (`what` is the subject.) 2. `Who did Mia invite?` (`who` is the object.)

**49.3** Didn’t you hear the announcement?
