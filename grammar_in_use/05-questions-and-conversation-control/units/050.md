# Unit 50 — Indirect questions and reported questions

## A

At a station, a traveller asks a staff member:

> “Could you tell me where platform 6 is?”

The direct question is `Where is platform 6?` After `Could you tell me`, `Do you know`, `I wonder`, or `I don’t know`, it becomes part of a longer sentence. We use statement order:

> Do you know what time the bank opens?  
> I can’t remember where I parked.  
> Can you tell me how much it will cost?

We do not say `Do you know what time does the bank open?`

## B

The same rule matters with `do/does/did` questions. The auxiliary disappears inside the longer question, and the main verb carries the normal tense ending:

> What does this sign mean? → Please explain what this sign means.  
> Why did Lila leave? → I wonder why Lila left.

Use `if` or `whether` when there is no question word:

> I don’t know if the café is open.  
> Could you check whether anyone called?

`If` here means “whether”, not a condition.

## C

Reported questions also use statement order:

> The interviewer asked me where I lived.  
> My neighbour wanted to know what I was doing.  
> They asked whether I could stay late.

When the reporting verb is in the past, the reported verb often moves back: `are` → `were`, `have been working` → `had been working`, `can` → `could`. A current fact can sometimes stay unchanged when that is clearer:

> She asked where I work. *(my present job is still the same)*

## Exercises

### 50.1

Complete the polite questions.

1. `Where is the nearest pharmacy?` → Could you tell me ... ?  
2. `What time does the film start?` → Do you know ... ?

### 50.2

Report these questions from yesterday.

1. “Why are you learning Spanish?”  
2. “Did you lock the door?”

### 50.3

Correct: `I don’t know where did they go.`

<summary>Answers</summary>

**50.1** 1. Could you tell me where the nearest pharmacy is? 2. Do you know what time the film starts?

**50.2** 1. She/he asked why I was learning Spanish. 2. She/he asked whether (or if) I had locked the door.

**50.3** I don’t know where they went. An embedded question uses statement order.
