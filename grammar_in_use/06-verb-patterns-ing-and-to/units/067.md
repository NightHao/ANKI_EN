# Unit 67 — Perception verbs + bare infinitive or `-ing`

## A

Study this situation:

> From the balcony, I watched a cyclist stop, unlock the gate, and ride into the courtyard.

After `see`, `hear`, `watch`, `listen to`, and `feel`, use `object + base verb` when you perceive a complete action: *We heard the door slam.* *I saw the child drop the glass.* The action happened, and you perceived it as a whole.

## B

Use `object + -ing` when you perceive an action in progress: *I saw the child carrying the glass.* *Can you hear the neighbours arguing?* You notice the action while it is happening; you do not say whether you saw its beginning or end.

`smell` and `find` are also common with `-ing`: *Can you smell something burning?* *We found the dog sleeping under the table.*

## C

Compare the focus:

| Complete event | Activity in progress |
|---|---|
| *I watched Lina cross the street.* | *I watched Lina crossing the street.* |
| *We heard the guests leave.* | *We heard the guests talking outside.* |

Sometimes either form is possible, especially when the distinction is unimportant: *I have never seen him dance/dancing.*

## Exercises

### 67.1

Choose the correct form.

1. I heard someone ___ the window, then the room went quiet. (close)
2. We could hear the rain ___ on the roof. (fall)
3. She watched the bus ___ around the corner. (turn)
4. I found my keys ___ under a pile of papers. (lie)

### 67.2

Rewrite using a perception verb.

1. A glass fell from the shelf. I saw it. (`see`)
2. The baby was crying upstairs. We could hear it. (`hear`)
3. A strange smell was coming from the oven. (`smell`)

<summary>Answers</summary>

**67.1** 1. *close* 2. *falling* 3. *turn* 4. *lying*.

**67.2** 1. *I saw a glass fall from the shelf.* 2. *We could hear the baby crying upstairs.* 3. *We could smell something burning in the oven.*
