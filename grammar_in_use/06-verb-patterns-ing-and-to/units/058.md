# Unit 58 — Verb + `-ing` or `to`: likes and wishes

## A

Study this situation:

> Nia works from home now. “I like working here,” she says. “But I like to answer messages before lunch, so I do not lose track of them.”

After `like`, `love`, and `hate`, both `-ing` and `to + verb` can describe repeated actions: *I love meeting new colleagues.* / *I love to meet new colleagues.*

Use `-ing` when you describe an existing experience or activity and your feeling about it: *He hates living beside the motorway.* He lives there now. *Do you like being your own boss?*

## B

`I like doing` often means “I enjoy it”: *I like cooking for friends.* `I like to do` can emphasise a choice, routine, or what you think is good practice: *I like to check the route before I leave.* Enjoyment is not the main point.

`enjoy` and `mind` take `-ing`, not an infinitive: *We enjoy walking by the river.* *Do you mind waiting five minutes?*

## C

`would like`, `would love`, `would hate`, and `would prefer` usually refer to a particular wish, so they take `to + verb`: *I’d like to speak to the manager.* *Would you prefer to sit outside?*

Compare *I like to swim* (a general preference) with *I’d like to swim this afternoon* (a wish now).

For a past opportunity that did not happen, use `would like/love/hate/prefer + to have + past participle`: *I’d love to have seen the exhibition, but I was away.*

## Exercises

### 58.1

Complete the sentences with the correct form.

1. I enjoy ___ recipes from other countries. (try)
2. Would you mind ___ the window? (close)
3. We would like ___ a table for six. (book)
4. My aunt likes ___ the market early, before it gets busy. (visit)

### 58.2

Choose the best completion.

1. Leila is a doctor and is happy in her job. She likes ___ a doctor. (`being` / `to be`)
2. I missed the concert. I would have loved ___ it. (`to hear` / `to have heard`)
3. “Tea or coffee?” “I’d prefer ___, please.” (`tea` / `drinking tea`)

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**58.1** 1. *trying* 2. *closing* 3. *to book* 4. *to visit*; this expresses her usual choice or routine.

**58.2** 1. *being* 2. *to have heard* 3. *tea*.
