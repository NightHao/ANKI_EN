# Unit 53 — Verb + `-ing`

## A

At the end of a long week, two friends make plans:

> “Do you fancy staying in?”  
> “Yes. I’d enjoy watching something quiet.”

After verbs such as `enjoy`, `mind`, `suggest`, `avoid`, `finish`, `consider`, `admit`, `deny`, `risk`, and `fancy`, we use an `-ing` form:

> She suggested taking the earlier bus.  
> Would you mind opening the window?  
> We avoided driving during the storm.

We do not say `She suggested to take the bus.` The negative is `not + -ing`:

> I enjoy not having an alarm at weekends.

## B

Several verb–particle combinations also take `-ing`:

> I gave up buying bottled water.  
> Don’t put off replying to them.  
> He carried on talking after the bell.  
> You keep leaving your phone on the train.

`Give up` means stop permanently; `put off` means delay; `go on/carry on` means continue. Learn each as one pattern.

## C

We can put a person or thing before the `-ing` action:

> I can’t imagine my uncle riding a scooter.  
> Sorry to keep you waiting.  
> Can you stop people parking here?

For an earlier completed action, `having + past participle` is possible, especially with `admit` and `regret`:

> She admitted having copied the file.

Usually simple `-ing` is enough: `She admitted copying the file.`

## D

`Suggest`, `recommend`, `admit`, and `deny` can also introduce a clause:

> I recommend that you take the train.  
> He denied that he had changed the booking.

This is different from `suggest somebody to do`, which we do not use.

## Exercises

### 53.1

Complete with the correct form.

1. I can’t imagine ___ (live) without music.  
2. Please stop ___ (interrupt) me.  
3. They denied ___ (break) the screen.

### 53.2

Choose the best completion.

1. We put off ___ the landlord. (`call` / `to call`)  
2. I don’t mind you ___ my charger. (`use` / `using`)

### 53.3

Correct: `My colleague suggested to meet after lunch.`

**53.1** 1. living 2. interrupting 3. breaking

**53.2** 1. calling 2. using

**53.3** My colleague suggested meeting after lunch. `Suggest` takes `-ing` or a that-clause.
