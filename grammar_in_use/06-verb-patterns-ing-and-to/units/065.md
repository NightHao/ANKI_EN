# Unit 65 — Adjective + infinitive

## A

Study this situation:

> “This instruction leaflet is difficult to follow,” said Ben. “The diagrams are easy to understand, though.”

With adjectives such as `easy`, `hard`, `safe`, `dangerous`, `cheap`, and `impossible`, we can use `adjective + to + verb`: *Is this soup safe to eat?* *The final question was impossible to answer.*

The object is already the subject in this form: *The soup is safe to eat* (not *safe to eat it*). Similarly, *He is interesting to talk to* means talking to him is interesting.

## B

Use `It is + adjective + of + person + to + verb` to judge someone’s action: *It was generous of Eva to lend me her car.* *It was careless of me to leave the door open.*

## C

Some adjective + infinitive patterns show a reaction to news or an event: *We were delighted to hear the result.* *I was sorry to miss your call.* *They were relieved to arrive safely.*

## D

An infinitive can follow `the first`, `the last`, `the next`, `the only`, and similar expressions: *Jun was the first to notice the error.* *She is the only person to know the code.*

## E

Use `sure`, `likely`, or `bound + to + verb` for expectation: *The roads are likely to be crowded tonight.* *With that preparation, he is bound to do well.*

## Exercises

### 65.1

Rewrite without changing the meaning.

1. It is difficult to pronounce this word. → This word is ___ .
2. It is unsafe to cross this road at night. → This road is ___ .
3. It was kind of Inez to wait for us. → Inez was ___ .
4. Nobody else arrived early. → Paulo was the only person ___ .

### 65.2

Choose the correct completion.

1. I was pleased ___ from you. (`to hear` / `hearing`)
2. The package is too large ___ in this locker. (`to fit` / `to fit it`)
3. It is likely ___ tomorrow. (`to rain` / `raining`)
4. It was silly of us ___ the instructions. (`to ignore` / `ignoring`)

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**65.1** 1. *difficult to pronounce* 2. *unsafe to cross at night* 3. *kind to wait for us* 4. *to arrive early*.

**65.2** 1. *to hear* 2. *to fit* 3. *to rain* 4. *to ignore*.
