# Unit 66 — Infinitive versus preposition + `-ing` after adjectives

## A

Study this situation:

> Tariq did not tell the team about the error because he was afraid to upset them. He was afraid of making the situation worse.

`afraid to + verb` means you avoid an action because of a possible bad result: *I was afraid to touch the loose wire.* `afraid of + -ing` means you fear that something will happen: *I was afraid of getting an electric shock.*

The two ideas often belong together: *She was afraid to complain because she was afraid of losing her job.*

## B

`interested in + -ing` means wanting to do an activity or considering it: *Are you interested in joining the course?* `interested to + verb` means that information or an event is interesting to you: *I was interested to learn that the museum is free on Fridays.*

## C

Use `sorry for + -ing` to apologise for an earlier action: *I’m sorry for interrupting you.* Use `sorry to + verb` when you regret news or when you politely interrupt now: *I’m sorry to hear about your loss.* *I’m sorry to disturb you, but could I ask something?*

## D

Learn the whole pattern rather than assuming that every `to` is an infinitive marker:

| Infinitive | Preposition + `-ing` |
|---|---|
| *I plan to leave.* | *I am thinking of leaving.* |
| *She promised to call.* | *She insisted on calling.* |
| *We failed to agree.* | *We succeeded in agreeing.* |

## Exercises

### 66.1

Choose the correct form.

1. We were afraid (`to miss` / `of missing`) the last train.
2. I was afraid (`to ask` / `of asking`) for a refund because the manager looked angry.
3. Is she interested in (`apply` / `applying`) for the position?
4. I would be interested (`to know` / `in knowing`) what happened next.

### 66.2

Complete the sentences.

1. I am sorry ___ ___ so late last night. (for / call)
2. We were sorry ___ that your flight was cancelled. (hear)
3. I’m sorry ___ you, but this seat is reserved. (bother)
4. They prevented us ___ ___ the building. (from / enter)

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**66.1** 1. *of missing* 2. *to ask* 3. *applying* 4. *to know*.

**66.2** 1. *for calling* 2. *to hear* 3. *to bother* 4. *from entering*.
