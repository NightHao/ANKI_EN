# Unit 55 — Verb + object + `to`

## A

Before a presentation, a manager asks a colleague:

> “Would you like me to introduce you first?”

After `want`, `expect`, `ask`, `help`, `would like`, and `would prefer`, we can use an object before the infinitive:

> I want you to check the figures.  
> We expected the room to be full.  
> Would you prefer me to wait outside?

We do not normally say `I want that you check the figures.`

## B

These verbs also use `object + to + verb`: `tell`, `advise`, `remind`, `warn`, `invite`, `encourage`, `persuade`, `get`, `force`, `teach`, `allow`, and `enable`.

> Please remind me to renew my pass.  
> The guide warned us not to touch the statue.  
> Her coach encouraged her to apply.  
> The lift enables visitors to reach every floor.

Passive sentences keep `to`:

> We were warned not to enter.  
> Are guests allowed to take photographs?

`Suggest` is different: say `She suggested that I ask`, or `She suggested asking`—not `suggested me to ask`.

## C

After `make` and `let`, use the base verb, without `to`:

> The noise made me lose concentration.  
> Let her finish her point.

In the passive, `make` changes to `be made to`:

> We were made to wait outside.

`Help` allows both forms: `help me carry` and `help me to carry`.

## Exercises

### 55.1

Complete the sentences.

1. My doctor advised me ___ (rest).  
2. They don’t allow visitors ___ (park) here.  
3. I’d like you ___ (meet) my team.

### 55.2

Choose the correct form.

1. The film made me `cry / to cry`.  
2. We were made `wait / to wait`.  
3. Let him `explain / to explain`.

### 55.3

Correct: `Nora suggested us to leave early.`

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**55.1** 1. to rest 2. to park 3. to meet

**55.2** 1. cry 2. to wait 3. explain

**55.3** Nora suggested leaving early. You can also say `Nora suggested that we leave early.`
