# Unit 59 — `prefer` and `would rather`

## A

Study this conversation:

> “Do you normally work with music?”
> “No, I prefer silence to music.”

For general preferences, use `prefer A to B`: *Ravi prefers autumn to summer.* With actions, use `prefer doing A to doing B`: *I prefer cycling to driving in the city.* You can also say `prefer doing A rather than doing B`.

Another general pattern is `prefer to do A rather than do B`: *I prefer to cook rather than order food.*

## B

For one particular choice, use `would prefer to + verb`: *It is late; I’d prefer to take a taxi.* We do not normally say `I’d prefer taking a taxi` for this specific choice.

`would rather + base verb` has a similar meaning: *I’d rather take a taxi.* There is no `to` after `rather`. The negative is `would rather not`: *I’d rather not discuss it at work.*

You can compare choices: *I’d rather read than watch another episode.* / *I’d prefer to read rather than watch another episode.*

## C

To say what you want another person to do, use `would rather + person + past form`, even though the meaning is present or future: *I’d rather you stayed for dinner.* *Would you rather we met on Friday?*

For a negative request, use `would rather + person + didn’t + verb`: *I’d rather you didn’t park outside the entrance.*

## Exercises

### 59.1

Rewrite using `prefer`.

1. In general, Ana chooses paper books, not ebooks.
2. In general, I choose taking the stairs, not using the lift.
3. In general, they choose to travel by ferry, not fly.

### 59.2

Complete with the correct form.

1. “Shall we eat out?” “I’d rather ___ at home.” (cook)
2. “Can I tell Jo?” “I’d rather you ___ her yet.” (not / tell)
3. I’d prefer ___ until the rain stops. (wait)
4. I would rather walk ___ look for parking. (than / rather than)

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**59.1** 1. *Ana prefers paper books to ebooks.* 2. *I prefer taking the stairs to using the lift.* 3. *They prefer to travel by ferry rather than fly.*

**59.2** 1. *cook* 2. *didn’t tell* 3. *to wait* 4. *than*.
