# Unit 57 — Verb + `-ing` or `to`: try, need, help

## A

Study this exchange:

> “The login page still will not open.”
> “Try clearing the browser cache.”
> “I tried to clear it, but I do not know where the setting is.”

`try to do` means make an effort or attempt something. The speaker may fail: *Mira tried to lift the suitcase, but it was too heavy.*

`try doing` means do something as a possible solution or experiment. *Try using a different cable; it may charge faster.* The action is suggested to discover whether it helps.

## B

`need to do` says who has a necessary action to perform: *I need to renew my passport this week.*

With things, `need + -ing` means “need to be done”: *The gate needs painting.* = *The gate needs to be painted.* The gate does not paint itself.

Compare:

| Person responsible | Thing receiving the action |
|---|---|
| *We need to replace the lock.* | *The lock needs replacing.* |

## C

After `help`, both `help + object + base verb` and `help + object + to + verb` are usual: *Could you help me carry these boxes?* / *Could you help me to carry these boxes?* You can also omit the object: *Several neighbours helped tidy up.*

`can’t help doing` has a different meaning: you cannot stop yourself. *The film was sad, but I could not help smiling at the last scene.* We do not say `can’t help to smile` with this meaning.

## Exercises

### 57.1

Choose `to + verb` or `-ing`.

1. The printer has stopped. Try ___ (turn) it off and on again.
2. I tried ___ (explain) the map, but nobody understood me.
3. This jacket needs ___ (wash).
4. We need ___ (wash) the jackets before the trip.

### 57.2

Complete with the correct form of the verb in brackets.

1. Can you help me ___ this table upstairs? (move)
2. Everyone helped ___ the room after the workshop. (clear)
3. When I hear that song, I cannot help ___. (sing)

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**57.1** 1. *turning* 2. *to explain* 3. *washing* 4. *to wash*.

**57.2** 1. *move* or *to move* 2. *clear* or *to clear* 3. *singing*.
