# Unit 61 — `be/get used to`

## A

Study this situation:

> In her first week at a bakery, Lena found the 5 a.m. start exhausting. Three months later, she says, “I’m used to getting up early now.”

`be used to + noun/-ing` means something is familiar and no longer strange or difficult: *I’m used to the traffic here.* *He is used to sharing an office.*

The phrase can describe a present state or a past state: *We were not used to such dry weather.*

## B

`get used to + noun/-ing` describes the change from unfamiliar to familiar: *You will soon get used to the new software.* *It took us a while to get used to speaking online all day.*

Use `get used to` for the process, and `be used to` for the result. *At first I was not used to the keyboard; now I am used to it.*

In both phrases, `to` is a preposition. Therefore it is followed by a noun, pronoun, or `-ing`, not `to + verb`: *She is used to working weekends* (not *used to work weekends* in this meaning).

## C

Do not confuse these with `used to + base verb`. `used to do` describes a regular past situation that is no longer true: *I used to take the bus to school, but now I cycle.*

Compare: *I’m used to taking the bus* means it is normal for me now. *I used to take the bus* describes an old habit.

## Exercises

### 61.1

Complete each sentence.

1. The flat is near a railway line, but we are used to ___ the trains. (hear)
2. It was difficult at first, but Omar soon got used to ___ in a larger team. (work)
3. I have never worked nights, so I am not used to ___ awake then. (stay)
4. The new schedule feels unfamiliar now, but it will feel normal soon. (Use `get used to`.)

### 61.2

Choose the meaning of each sentence.

1. *I used to wear glasses.* Is it an old habit, or something familiar now?
2. *I’m used to wearing glasses.* Is it an old habit, or something familiar now?
3. Correct: *We are used to work under pressure.*

**61.1** 1. *hearing* 2. *working* 3. *staying* 4. *You will get used to the new schedule.*

**61.2** 1. An old habit (perhaps no longer true). 2. Something familiar now. 3. *We are used to working under pressure.*
