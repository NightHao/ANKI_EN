# Unit 92 — Relative clauses 1: `who`, `that` and `which`

## A

Study these two sentences:

> Several neighbours came to the meeting. They all signed the letter.

We can join them:

> Every neighbour **who came to the meeting** signed the letter.

A relative clause tells us which person or thing we mean, or what kind of person or
thing we mean. In the example, **who came to the meeting** identifies the neighbours.

Use **who** for people:

- “The woman who runs the bakery is retiring.”
- “I like people who say what they mean.”
- “Do you know anyone who repairs cameras?”

**That** is also possible for people in this type of clause: “the woman that runs the
bakery”. Do not use **which** for a person.

## B

For things, use **that** or **which**:

- “The bus that goes to the airport stops here.”
- “The book which explains the method is online.”
- “My phone has an app that translates signs.”

In identifying clauses like these, **that** is often natural in conversation. Later
units explain cases where only **which** is possible.

## C

The relative word replaces the subject inside its clause. Do not add another subject
pronoun:

- “I met a nurse. She works at the clinic.”
- “I met a nurse who works at the clinic.”  
  (not “a nurse who she works”)

Similarly, say “Where are the keys that were on the desk?” (not “the keys that they
were on the desk?”).

## D

**What** already means *the thing(s) that*:

- “What happened surprised me.”  
  (= the thing that happened)

When a noun or a word such as **everything** is already present, use **that**, not
**what**:

- “Everything that happened was recorded.”
- “The message that arrived this morning was incomplete.”

## Exercises

### 92.1

Join the sentences with **who, that** or **which**.

1. A woman called. She found my wallet.
2. We use a machine. It checks every ticket.
3. I avoid websites. They play music automatically.

### 92.2

Correct the sentences.

1. The colleague which sits beside me is helpful.
2. Where is the parcel that it arrived yesterday?
3. Everything what he told us was accurate.

### 92.3

Complete with **what** or **that**.

1. I did not understand ___ she meant.
2. The explanation ___ she gave was clear.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**92.1** 1. The woman who/that called found my wallet. 2. We use a machine
that/which checks every ticket. 3. I avoid websites that/which play music
automatically.

**92.2** 1. The colleague who/that sits beside me is helpful. 2. Where is the
parcel that arrived yesterday? 3. Everything that he told us was accurate.

**92.3** 1. what 2. that.
