# Unit 129 — Noun + preposition

## A

Study these notes:

> There is no **need for** another meeting, but we need a **solution to** the delay.

Some nouns have fixed following prepositions. Use **demand/need/excuse/reason for**; **picture/map/photo/plan of**; **increase/decrease/fall/rise in**; and **cause of**.

> A map of the district showed a rise in traffic.  
> Nobody knows the cause of the outage.

## B

Use **relationship/contact/connection with** for a link to someone or something. Use **connection/difference between** two things: *a difference between the two versions.*

## C

Use **solution/key/answer/reply/reaction to**, **damage to**, **invitation to**, and **attitude to/towards**. `Solution of` is not the usual phrase.

## D

Use **advantage/disadvantage of + noun**: *the advantage of a flexible schedule*. For an activity, say **advantages in/to + `-ing`**: *advantages to working remotely*.

## Exercises

### 129.1

Complete with the correct preposition.

1. There has been a sharp fall ___ demand.
2. Did you get a reply ___ your message?
3. The key ___ the storage room is missing.
4. There is little contact ___ their neighbours.

### 129.2

Complete the paired phrase.

1. A connection ___ two events; contact ___ a colleague.
2. The cause ___ the leak; a solution ___ the leak.
3. An advantage ___ living alone; advantages ___ living alone.

### 129.3

Correct the noun phrase.

1. We need a solution of this problem.
2. There was damage of the roof.
3. She sent an invitation for the reception.
