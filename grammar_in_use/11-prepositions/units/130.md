# Unit 130 — Adjective + preposition: feelings and reactions

## A

Study these thank-you messages:

> It was very kind **of you** to help. You have always been kind **to me**.

Use **nice/kind/generous/polite/rude/cruel of somebody to do something** to judge an action: *It was careless of me to forget.* Use these adjectives **to somebody** for behaviour towards a person: *They were polite to the new staff member.*

## B

Use **pleased/satisfied/happy/delighted/disappointed with** something received or experienced. Use **surprised/shocked/amazed/upset at/by** something. Use **angry/annoyed/upset about** a situation, and **angry/annoyed/furious with somebody for doing something**.

> I was disappointed with the service.  
> She was annoyed with me for cancelling.

## C

Other common patterns are **excited/worried/nervous about**, **impressed with/by**, **fed up/bored with**, and **tired of**.

## D

Use **sorry about** a situation, and **sorry for/about** something you did: *Sorry about the noise; I am sorry for interrupting.* But **feel sorry for** means sympathy for a person in a bad situation.

## Exercises

### 130.1

Complete with the correct preposition.

1. It was generous ___ you to offer a lift.
2. They have been very friendly ___ us.
3. I am nervous ___ tomorrow’s exam.
4. We were impressed ___ her presentation.

### 130.2

Complete the sentence.

1. I am annoyed ___ Sam ___ arriving late.
2. I am sorry ___ the broken glass.
3. I feel sorry ___ the families affected.

### 130.3

Choose the natural completion.

1. We are tired (of waiting / with waiting).
2. The guests were delighted (with / at) the meal.
3. I was shocked (at / to) the price.
