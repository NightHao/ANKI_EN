# Unit 125 — `in`, `at`, and `on` for institutions, events, and transport

## A

Study this message:

> I am **at work** now, but my brother is **in hospital** and our children are **at school**.

Use **in bed, in hospital,** and **in prison**. Use **at home, at work, at school, at university,** and **at college**. We can say *be home* or *be at home*, but say *work/study/eat at home*.

## B

Use **at** for an event: *at a concert, at a wedding, at a conference*. With many buildings, both `in` and `at` are possible, but the focus changes. `At` often means the activity or location; `in` focuses on the building itself.

> We ate at the restaurant.  
> It was very warm in the restaurant.

Say **at someone’s house**, **at the dentist’s**, **at the airport**, and **at the station**.

## C

Use **in** for cities, towns, and villages: *in Nairobi; in a village*. Use **at** when a town is a stop or point on a journey: *The train stops at York.*

## D

Use **on** a bus, train, plane, ship, bike, motorbike, or horse; use **in** a car or taxi: *on the train; in a taxi*. 

## Exercises

### 125.1

Complete with `in`, `at`, or `on`.

1. I saw her ___ a conference last week.
2. Their parents live ___ a small town near Lyon.
3. We arrived ___ a taxi.
4. The children are still ___ bed.

### 125.2

Choose the natural completion.

1. The meeting was (in / at) the company headquarters.
2. The heating is broken (at / in) the company headquarters.
3. This express train does not stop (in / at) Bristol.

### 125.3

Correct the preposition where necessary.

1. I left my wallet in the bus.
2. We stayed at a very cold hotel.
3. She is studying in university.
