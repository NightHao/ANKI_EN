# Unit 127 — Other uses of `in`, `on`, and `at`

## A

Study this comment:

> We ate **in the shade** because sitting **in the sun** was too hot.

Use **in** with conditions and surroundings: *in the rain, in the dark, in fog, in bad weather*. We also say *in Italian, in dollars, in love, in a good mood,* and *in my opinion*.

> In my opinion, the instructions are clear.  
> How much is this price in euros?

## B

Use **on** with media and certain states: *on TV, on the radio, on the phone, on fire, on purpose, on the whole*. `On purpose` means intentionally.

We also say **on holiday, on a trip/tour/cruise, on business, on strike,** and **on a diet**. You can alternatively say *go to Greece for a holiday*.

## C

Use **at** for age, speed, temperature, and measurement: *at the age of twelve; at 90 km/h; at 20 degrees; at an altitude of 3,000 metres*.

## Exercises

### 127.1

Complete with `in`, `on`, or `at`.

1. I do not like driving ___ heavy rain.
2. We heard the update ___ the radio.
3. The water is kept ___ 18°C.
4. She left home ___ the age of seventeen.

### 127.2

Choose the natural phrase.

1. I did not break it (by chance / on purpose).
2. They are away (on holiday / in holiday).
3. The workers are (on strike / in strike).

### 127.3

Complete each sentence.

1. The guidebook is available ___ three languages.
2. He always seems to be ___ a cheerful mood.
3. ___ the whole, the trip was successful.
