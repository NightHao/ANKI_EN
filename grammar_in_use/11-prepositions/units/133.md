# Unit 133 — Verb + preposition: `about`, `for`, `of`, and `after`

## A

Study a staff meeting:

> We talked **about** the delay and discussed a new plan.

Use **talk/read/know about** something and **do something about** a problem. Say **have a discussion about**, but **discuss something** (not *discuss about something*).

## B

Use **apply to** an organisation **for** a job/course, **ask somebody for** something, **look/search for** something, **wait for** somebody or an event, and **leave a place for** another place.

> I applied to three colleges for a place.  
> We left the office for the station.

## C

**Care for** can mean look after someone, or not like something: *I do not care for spicy food.* **Care about** means consider important: *She cares about animal welfare.* Before `what/where/how`, use `care` without a preposition: *I do not care what they think.*

## D

Use **take care of** and **look after** for responsibility or protection: *Could you look after my plants? I will take care of the booking.* Do not confuse **look for** (try to find) with **look after**.

## Exercises

### 133.1

Complete with the correct preposition or no preposition.

1. We discussed ___ the budget all morning.
2. Please ask the receptionist ___ a map.
3. I am waiting ___ the rain to stop.
4. They left Manchester ___ Leeds.

### 133.2

Choose the meaning of `care`.

1. “I do not care for olives.” (dislike / protect)
2. “He cares about his neighbours.” (thinks they matter / looks after them)
3. “I do not care where we eat.” What follows `care`?

### 133.3

Complete with `look for` or `look after`.

1. Can you ___ my suitcase while I buy a ticket?
2. I am ___ a quieter apartment.
3. Who ___ you when you were ill?

<summary>Answers</summary>

**133.1** 1. no preposition  2. for  3. for  4. for.  
**133.2** 1. dislike  2. thinks they matter  3. `where` directly, with no preposition.  
**133.3** 1. look after  2. looking for  3. looked after.
