# Unit 131 — Adjective + preposition: ability, similarity, and relationships

## A

Study this self-description:

> I am good **at** fixing bicycles, but afraid **of** heights.

Use **afraid/scared/frightened/terrified of**; **good/bad/brilliant/hopeless at**; **fond/proud/ashamed/jealous of**; **aware/conscious of**; and **capable/incapable of**. We also say **typical of**: *It is typical of her to arrive early.*

## B

Use **similar to**, **dependent on** (but **independent of**), **interested in**, **famous for**, and **certain/sure of/about**. `Different from` and `different to` are both used.

## C

Use **crowded with**, **keen on**, **responsible for**, and **full/short of**:

> The hall was crowded with visitors.  
> Who is responsible for the mistake?  
> We are short of time but full of ideas.

## D

Use **married/engaged to** a person: *Nora is married to Diego.* **Married with** introduces children: *Nora is married with two children.*

## Exercises

### 131.1

Complete with the correct preposition.

1. She is very proud ___ her work.
2. Are you interested ___ architecture?
3. The twins are similar ___ each other.
4. I am not sure ___ the address.

### 131.2

Complete the sentence.

1. The room was crowded ___ students.
2. Our project is dependent ___ approval.
3. He is hopeless ___ remembering names.
4. We are short ___ chairs.

### 131.3

Correct the preposition.

1. She is married with a doctor.
2. He is famous of his paintings.
3. We are keen in trying the new restaurant.

**131.2** 1. with  2. on  3. at  4. of.  
**131.3** 1. She is married to a doctor. 2. He is famous for his paintings. 3. We are keen on trying the new restaurant.
