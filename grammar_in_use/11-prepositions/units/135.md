# Unit 135 — Verb + preposition: `of`, `for`, `from`, and `on`

## A

Study a school report:

> The student was accused **of** copying, but the class congratulated her **on** finishing the project.

Use **accuse/suspect somebody of**, **approve/disapprove of**, **die of/from** an illness, and **consist of**. Use **congratulate/compliment somebody on** something.

## B

Use **thank/forgive/apologise (to somebody) for** something. Use **pay for** a thing or service, but **pay a bill/rent/tax/a fine** without `for`.

> I paid for the meal, then paid the parking fine.

The distinction is about the noun after `pay`, not about formality. *Pay for repairs* is normal because repairs are a service; *pay the bill* names the bill directly.

## C

Use **suffer from** an illness and **protect somebody/something from** danger. Use **live on** money or food: *They can live on a small pension.*

## D

Use **blame somebody/something for** a problem, but **blame a problem on** somebody or something. Use **depend on/rely on**. Before `how/when/where`, `depend` can be used with or without `on`.

## Exercises

### 135.1

Complete with the correct preposition.

1. The meal consists ___ four courses.
2. I congratulated her ___ her promotion.
3. Please forgive me ___ being late.
4. The plants need protection ___ frost.

### 135.2

Choose the natural phrase.

1. We paid (for the tickets / the tickets for).
2. I paid (for my rent / my rent).
3. They blamed the delay (on / for) the weather.

### 135.3

Complete the sentence.

1. Whether we leave today depends ___ the forecast.
2. Do not rely ___ the last bus.
3. She suffers ___ migraines.
