# Unit 43 — Passive 2: `be done`, `been done`, and `being done`

## A

Study these updates:

> “The booking has been cancelled.”  
> “The kitchen is being painted this week.”

Use `have/has been + past participle` for present perfect passive: *The invoice has been paid.* / *Have you ever been bitten by a jellyfish?* Use `had been + past participle` for past perfect passive: *The hall had been decorated before the guests arrived.*

## B

Use `am/is/are being + past participle` for an action happening now: *A new cycle lane is being built.* Use `was/were being + past participle` for an action in progress at a past time: *We were being watched as we crossed the square.*

## C

After a modal or infinitive, keep the passive form: `to be done`, `must be done`, `can be seen`: *The error must be corrected.* / *The sign can be read from the road.* / *The room will be cleaned later.*

For an earlier passive action, use `to have been + past participle`: *The parcel may have been sent to the wrong address.* / *The problem appears to have been solved.*

## Exercises

### 43.1

Complete with the correct passive form.

1. The invitations ___ already ___ (send).
2. The bridge ___ (repair) when we drove past yesterday.
3. Something must ___ (do) before the deadline.
4. The missing files may ___ (delete) by mistake.

### 43.2

Rewrite in the passive.

1. They are widening the footpath.
2. Someone had locked the door before we arrived.
3. They should have checked the figures.

### 43.3

Choose the correct form.

1. The suspect **(is following / is being followed)** by officers.
2. The old theatre **(will be restored / will have restored)** next year.
3. The documents seem **(to have been lost / to have lost)**.
