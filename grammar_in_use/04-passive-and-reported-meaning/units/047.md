# Unit 47 — Reported speech 1: `he said that ...`

## A

Study this report after a conversation:

> Direct: “I’m feeling unwell.”  
> Reported: Mira said that she was feeling unwell.

Reported speech tells us what somebody said without repeating their exact words. After a past reporting verb, the verb usually moves back: `am/is → was`, `are → were`, `will → would`, `can → could`, `have/has → had`, and present simple → past simple. `That` is optional: *Mira said (that) she was feeling unwell.*

## B

Pronouns and time words change to the reporter’s viewpoint: *“I’ll call you when I get back”* becomes *She said she would call me when she got back.*

## C

Past simple can stay past simple or move further back to past perfect: *Oren said he missed the bus.* / *Oren said he had missed the bus.* Both can be possible when reporting a past event.

## Exercises

### 47.1

Report the statements.

1. “I cannot attend on Friday,” Kai said.
2. “My parents are well,” Nessa said.
3. “I will send you the link tomorrow,” Leo said to me.

### 47.2

Change the tense and reference words.

1. “I have lost my card,” Uma said yesterday.
2. “We are leaving here now,” the visitors said.
3. “I saw the film last week,” Olin said.

### 47.3

Choose the acceptable reported form.

1. “I woke up late.” → She said she **(woke up / had woken up)** late.
2. “I can help you.” → He said he **(can / could)** help me.
3. “I will call you.” → She said she **(will / would)** call me.
