# Unit 46 — `have something done`

## A

Study this repair:

> “Did you fix the screen yourself?”  
> “No, I had it repaired.”

`Have + object + past participle` means that you arrange for another person to do a service: *We had the windows replaced last winter.* / *She is having her portrait painted.* It does not mean that the subject did the work personally.

The object comes before the past participle: *Where do you have your bike serviced?* / *I do not like having my photo taken.*

## B

`Get something done` has the same service meaning and is common in conversation: *I need to get my watch repaired.* / *You should get your eyes checked.*

## C

The same form can describe an unpleasant event that happens to someone or their possessions: *We had our bags searched at the airport.* / *He had his arm broken in a match.* / *Have you ever had a phone stolen?* Here, it does not mean the person arranged the event.

## Exercises

### 46.1

Rewrite to show that another person did the work.

1. A technician repaired my laptop.
2. A stylist cut Lian’s hair.
3. A builder is painting our fence.

### 46.2

Put the words in order.

1. (have / your / checked / eyes / should / you)
2. (had / last month / our / cleaned / carpets / we)
3. (get / as soon as possible / this document / translated / need to / I)

### 46.3

Choose the meaning: arranged service or something that happened to the person.

1. Farid had his wallet stolen on the train.
2. We had our kitchen painted in June.
3. I had my passport checked at the border.
