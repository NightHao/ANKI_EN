# Unit 48 — Reported speech 2

## A

Study this unchanged fact:

> Direct: “My new flat is tiny.”  
> Reported: Daria said that her new flat is tiny.

We do not always move a verb back in reported speech. If the situation is still true, the present form can remain: *Niko told me that he wants to change jobs next year.* A past form is also possible: *Niko told me that he wanted to change jobs next year.*

If the situation has ended or is clearly not true now, use past: *He left quickly and said that he had to go.* / *I met Toma outside, although Rina had said he was in hospital.*

## B

Use `tell + person` when you name the listener: *Daria told me that the shop had closed.* Use `say` without a listener: *Daria said that the shop had closed.* We can say `say something to somebody`: *What did you say to the guard?*

## C

For instructions, use `tell + person + to + verb`: *The nurse told me to rest.* For a negative instruction, use `not to`: *I told him not to worry.* For a request, use `ask + person + to + verb`: *She asked me to carry the box.*

## Exercises

### 48.1

Choose present or past in the report.

1. “My job is stressful,” Eva said. (It is still stressful.)
2. “I have to leave,” Ken said as he walked out.
3. “The shop opens at nine,” Ro said. (This is still true.)

### 48.2

Complete with `say` or `tell`.

1. What did you ___ to the receptionist?
2. Pasha ___ me that the train was delayed.
3. Do not ___ anyone what I ___ you.
4. She ___ goodbye to us and left.

### 48.3

Report the instruction or request.

1. “Please wait outside,” the officer said to us.
2. “Don’t touch the switch,” I said to Mel.
3. “Can you help me move this chair?” Inez said to me.
