# Unit 42 — Passive 1: `is done` and `was done`

## A

Study these descriptions:

> Active: “A local architect designed the library.”  
> Passive: “The library was designed in 1998.”

Active sentences focus on the doer. Passive sentences focus on what receives the action. We use the passive when the doer is unknown, unimportant, or not the topic: *Several laptops were stolen last night.* / *Is this corridor cleaned every day?*

Add `by` when the agent matters: *The library was designed by a local architect.*

## B

The passive form is `be + past participle`: *is repaired, are delivered, was damaged, were invited.* Many participles end in `-ed`, but some are irregular: *built, made, stolen, seen.*

## C

Choose the tense in `be`:

| Active | Passive |
|---|---|
| *They collect the bins every Tuesday.* | *The bins are collected every Tuesday.* |
| *They collected the bins yesterday.* | *The bins were collected yesterday.* |

Use `am/is/are + past participle` for present simple and `was/were + past participle` for past simple. Compare *The vase broke* (it happened) with *The vase was broken* (somebody broke it).

## Exercises

### 42.1

Complete with the present or past passive.

1. These shoes ___ (make) in Portugal.
2. The main road ___ (close) after the storm yesterday.
3. How ___ this word ___ (pronounce)?
4. We ___ (invite) to the ceremony last month.

### 42.2

Rewrite in the passive.

1. Someone delivers the parcels every morning.
2. They cancelled the match because of fog.
3. Someone found my keys on the bus.

### 42.3

Choose active or passive.

1. The company **(employs / is employed)** 500 people.
2. 500 people **(employ / are employed)** by the company.
3. My bag **(disappeared / was disappeared)** from the café.
4. My bag **(stole / was stolen)** from the café.
