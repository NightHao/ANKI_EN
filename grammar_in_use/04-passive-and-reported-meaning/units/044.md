# Unit 44 — Passive 3

## A

Study this gift:

> Active: “The museum gave us free tickets.”  
> Passive: “We were given free tickets.”

With verbs that take two objects, such as `give`, `offer`, `ask`, `pay`, `show`, and `tell`, either object can lead a passive sentence: *I was offered a refund.* / *A refund was offered to me.* The person-focused form is especially common: *We were shown the new system.*

## B

The passive after `-ing` is `being + past participle`: *I dislike being interrupted.* / *She remembers being taken to the airport.* / *We left without being seen.* It focuses on what somebody did to the person or thing.

## C

Say *I was born in 1996* and *Where were you born?* for one person’s past birth. For a general present fact, use *are born*: *Thousands of babies are born here each year.*

## D

`Get` is an informal alternative to `be` when an event happens: *Nobody got hurt.* / *My bike got stolen.* / *I do not get paid much.* Do not use it for a permanent state: *She is liked by everyone* (not *gets liked*).

`Get married`, `get dressed`, and `get lost` are common expressions, but they are not passive in meaning.

## Exercises

### 44.1

Make two passive sentences where possible.

1. The guide showed us a map.
2. They offered Priya a new contract.

### 44.2

Complete with `being` and the verb.

1. I hate ___ (keep) waiting.
2. We entered without ___ (notice).
3. He is afraid of ___ (bite) by dogs.

### 44.3

Choose `be` or `get` in the correct form.

1. My grandfather ___ born in a coastal village.
2. Two people ___ injured in the collision yesterday.
3. The office ___ cleaned every evening.
4. I ___ lost on the way to the hotel.

44.1 1. We were shown a map. / A map was shown to us. 2. Priya was offered a new contract. / A new contract was offered to Priya.

44.2 1. being kept 2. being noticed 3. being bitten.

44.3 1. was 2. got/were 3. is 4. got.
