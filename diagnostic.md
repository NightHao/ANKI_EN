## Questions

1. Right now, I ___ from home.
   - A. work
   - B. am working

2. I ___ that film three times.
   - A. saw
   - B. have seen

3. We ___ dinner with Mia on Friday; the table is already booked.
   - A. are having
   - B. will have

4. If I had more time, I ___ another language.
   - A. learn
   - B. would learn

5. She has not replied. She ___ be busy.
   - A. might
   - B. must to

6. You ___ have told me earlier; I could have helped.
   - A. should
   - B. should to

7. The final decision ___ tomorrow.
   - A. will announce
   - B. will be announced

8. He said he ___ tired.
   - A. was
   - B. is being

9. Could you tell me where the station ___?
   - A. is
   - B. is it

10. You have met Nina before, ___?
    - A. haven't you
    - B. don't you

11. I avoid ___ email late at night.
    - A. checking
    - B. to check

12. I stopped ___ some water on the way home.
    - A. buying
    - B. to buy

13. Could you give me ___ advice?
    - A. an
    - B. some

14. I enjoyed ___ we visited near the lake.
    - A. the café
    - B. a café

15. The person ___ called earlier left no message.
    - A. who
    - B. which

16. The report ___ yesterday contains an error.
    - A. submitting
    - B. submitted

17. The instructions were ___, so everyone felt ___.
    - A. confusing / confused
    - B. confused / confusing

18. My new commute is much ___ than the old one.
    - A. shorter
    - B. more short

19. ___ the salary is lower, the job offers better training.
    - A. Although
    - B. Despite

20. I stayed near the station ___ I would not miss the first train.
    - A. so that
    - B. during

21. The meeting starts ___ nine ___ Monday.
    - A. at / on
    - B. in / at

22. It depends ___ how much time we have.
    - A. of
    - B. on

23. We had to ___ the meeting until Friday.
    - A. put off
    - B. put it

24. Please turn ___ before you leave.
    - A. the lights off
    - B. off them
