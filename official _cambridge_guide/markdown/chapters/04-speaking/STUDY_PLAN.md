# IELTS Speaking — 8-week study plan

[Back to chapter index](../README.md)

The Speaking test is the same for IELTS Academic and IELTS General Training.
This plan divides the four Speaking units into eight weeks so that every lesson
is followed by active speaking, recording, and review.

## How to study each week

Use four study sessions if possible:

1. **Learn:** Read the assigned sections and complete the exercises.
2. **Prepare:** Collect useful ideas and expressions for the assigned task.
3. **Speak:** Answer aloud under the relevant time conditions and record
   yourself.
4. **Review:** Listen to the recording, identify important problems, and answer
   the same questions again.

Do not write and memorise complete scripts. Use short notes, then practise
expressing the ideas naturally.

## Week 1 — Understand Part 1 and answer familiar questions

### Study

[Unit 1: The Speaking Test — Part 1](01-the-speaking-test-part-1.md)

- Section 1: Getting ready to speak
- Section 2: Part 1 — talking about familiar topics

### Focus

- understand how Part 1 works
- answer the question directly
- extend short answers with a reason, detail, or example
- talk naturally about familiar subjects

### Weekly output

- Practise at least twelve Part 1 questions across three familiar topics.
- Record one set of six questions without restarting any answer.
- Listen once and note where an answer was too short, unclear, or off-topic.

- [ ] Week 1 completed

---

## Week 2 — Control tense and complete a Part 1 mock

### Study

[Unit 1: The Speaking Test — Part 1](01-the-speaking-test-part-1.md)

- Section 3: Using the right tense — Grammatical Range

### Focus

- notice whether a question concerns the past, present, or future
- keep the main tense consistent
- use a range of structures without forcing complexity
- correct recurring grammatical mistakes after speaking

### Weekly output

- Answer six questions that require different time frames.
- Complete and record one **4–5 minute Part 1 mock**.
- Transcribe only the sentences containing important errors, correct them, and
  answer those questions again.

- [ ] Week 2 completed

---

## Week 3 — Understand Part 2 and learn to keep speaking

### Study

[Unit 2: Part 2 — Giving a talk](02-part-2-giving-a-talk.md)

- Section 1: Understanding the task

### Focus

- use the one-minute preparation time effectively
- address every point on the task card
- expand each point with details, reasons, or a short story
- continue speaking when the first idea is finished

### Weekly output

- Prepare notes for three Part 2 task cards, using one minute for each card.
- Give three recorded talks; aim for at least **90 seconds** each.
- Identify which prompts helped you add useful detail.

- [ ] Week 3 completed

---

## Week 4 — Organise and deliver a two-minute talk

### Study

[Unit 2: Part 2 — Giving a talk](02-part-2-giving-a-talk.md)

- Section 2: Improving Fluency and Coherence
- Section 3: Organising your notes and your talk

### Focus

- organise ideas in an easy-to-follow sequence
- use brief notes rather than complete sentences
- connect details without overusing memorised linking phrases
- recover from pauses and continue speaking

### Weekly output

- Complete three cycles of:
  - one minute of preparation
  - up to two minutes of speaking
  - a short review of fluency and organisation
- Repeat the weakest talk once without writing a script.

- [ ] Week 4 completed

---

## Week 5 — Develop answers to abstract Part 3 questions

### Study

[Unit 3: Part 3 — Talking about abstract topics](03-part-3-talking-about-abstract-topics.md)

- Section 1: Talking about abstract topics

### Focus

- move from personal experience to general ideas
- state a claim and explain why it is reasonable
- support an answer with an example or consequence
- compare groups, situations, or periods when relevant

### Weekly output

- Practise eight Part 3 questions on two topics.
- Build each answer with a clear claim, explanation, and example.
- Record four answers and identify any unsupported claims.

- [ ] Week 5 completed

---

## Week 6 — Express and defend opinions

### Study

[Unit 3: Part 3 — Talking about abstract topics](03-part-3-talking-about-abstract-topics.md)

- Section 2: Agreeing and disagreeing
- Section 3: Improving your Lexical Resource score

### Focus

- agree or disagree with appropriate qualification
- explain both sides before giving a position when useful
- paraphrase when the exact word is unavailable
- use topic vocabulary accurately rather than forcing rare words

### Weekly output

- Complete one recorded **4–5 minute Part 3 mock**.
- Choose three answers and replace vague words only where a more accurate word
  is already known.
- Repeat the mock questions while keeping the same ideas but expressing them
  more clearly.

- [ ] Week 6 completed

---

## Week 7 — Handle problems and improve pronunciation

### Study

[Unit 4: Checking, correcting and assessing](04-checking-correcting-and-assessing.md)

- Section 1: Dealing with problems
- Section 2: Pronunciation, intonation and chunking

### Focus

- ask for clarification when a question is unclear
- correct yourself without abandoning the answer
- group words into meaningful chunks
- use sentence stress and intonation to make meaning clear

### Weekly output

- Practise clarification and self-correction phrases aloud.
- Select one earlier recording and mark meaningful pauses in its transcript.
- Record the answer again with clearer chunking and sentence stress.
- Compare the two recordings for clarity, not accent imitation.

- [ ] Week 7 completed

---

## Week 8 — Assess yourself and take a full mock test

### Study

[Unit 4: Checking, correcting and assessing](04-checking-correcting-and-assessing.md)

- Section 3: Assessing yourself and improving your score

### Focus

- assess fluency and coherence, vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation
  separately
- identify recurring problems from recordings
- maintain performance across all three parts

### Weekly output

- Record one complete **11–14 minute Speaking mock**:
  - Part 1: 4–5 minutes
  - Part 2: one minute to prepare and up to two minutes to speak
  - Part 3: 4–5 minutes
- Review the recording once and collect no more than three priority weaknesses.
- Repeat only the weakest part of the mock.

- [ ] Week 8 completed

---

## After this plan

Complete one full recorded mock each week. Keep a short error log, but also
record successful expressions that you used naturally. Choose one recurring
weakness for focused practice between mocks.
