# General Training Writing — 8-week study plan

[Back to chapter index](../README.md)

This plan covers the four units relevant to IELTS General Training Writing.
Units 1–4 in this folder teach **Academic Writing Task 1**, so they are not part
of this plan.

## How to study each week

Use four study sessions if possible:

1. **Learn:** Read the assigned sections and complete the exercises.
2. **Review:** Check the answer key and record important mistakes.
3. **Produce:** Plan or write the week's letter or essay.
4. **Improve:** Review the response, correct it, and rewrite weak sections.

Do not move on after merely reading the lesson. Complete the weekly output
before marking the week as finished.

---

## Week 1 — Understand General Training Task 1

### Study

[Unit 5: General Training Writing Task 1 — A letter](05-general-training-writing-task-1-a-letter.md)

- Section 1: Understanding the task
- Section 2: Improving your score, through Exercise 2.4

### Focus

- recognise informal, semi-formal, and formal situations
- identify the purpose of a letter
- address all three bullet points
- choose an appropriate tone

### Weekly output

- Analyse three letter questions: one informal, one semi-formal, and one formal.
- For each question, identify the reader, purpose, tone, and content required by
  every bullet point.
- Write an opening and closing for each letter.

- [ ] Week 1 completed

---

## Week 2 — Write and correct a complete letter

### Study

[Unit 5: General Training Writing Task 1 — A letter](05-general-training-writing-task-1-a-letter.md)

- Section 2: Exercise 2.5 to the end
- Section 3: Checking and correcting

### Focus

- organise the letter into clear paragraphs
- use suitable openings and closings
- maintain the correct tone
- check tense, spelling, punctuation, and task coverage

### Weekly output

- Write one letter without a time limit.
- Use the checking guidance in Section 3 and revise it.
- Write a second letter in **20 minutes** using at least **150 words**.
- Record repeated mistakes in a short error log.

- [ ] Week 2 completed

---

## Week 3 — Understand and plan Task 2 essays

### Study

[Unit 6: Writing Task 2 — Getting ready to write](06-writing-task-2-getting-ready-to-write.md)

- Section 1: Understanding the task
- Section 2: Planning and organising your ideas, through Exercise 2.4

### Focus

- identify exactly what the question asks
- choose and maintain a clear position
- generate relevant reasons and examples
- exclude interesting ideas that do not answer the question

### Weekly output

- Analyse three different Task 2 questions.
- For each question, write your position, two main ideas, and one supporting
  example for each idea.
- Do not write a complete essay yet.

- [ ] Week 3 completed

---

## Week 4 — Organise and write a Task 2 essay

### Study

[Unit 6: Writing Task 2 — Getting ready to write](06-writing-task-2-getting-ready-to-write.md)

- Section 2: Exercise 2.5 to the end
- Section 3: Getting started — writing an introduction

### Focus

- arrange ideas into logical paragraphs
- write a direct introduction
- make every paragraph support your position
- support claims with explanations or examples

### Weekly output

- Create two complete essay plans.
- Turn one plan into an untimed essay of at least **250 words**.
- Check whether every paragraph has one clear purpose.
- Revise the introduction and the weakest body paragraph.

- [ ] Week 4 completed

---

## Week 5 — Connect ideas and avoid repetition

### Study

[Unit 7: Writing Task 2 — Expressing your ideas clearly](07-writing-task-2-expressing-your-ideas-clearly.md)

- Section 1: Linking ideas — cohesion
- Section 2: Lexical Resource — avoiding repetition

### Focus

- express logical relationships accurately
- use linking words only when their meaning fits
- refer back to earlier ideas clearly
- avoid mechanical repetition without forcing unusual synonyms

### Weekly output

- Take the essay from Week 4 and mark every linking expression and repeated
  word.
- Revise only the places where the connection is unclear or the repetition is
  distracting.
- Write one new body paragraph that includes a claim, explanation, and example.

- [ ] Week 5 completed

---

## Week 6 — Express a position and complete a timed essay

### Study

[Unit 7: Writing Task 2 — Expressing your ideas clearly](07-writing-task-2-expressing-your-ideas-clearly.md)

- Section 3: Expressing a personal view

### Focus

- state your position precisely
- distinguish your view from other people's views
- maintain the same position throughout the essay
- write a conclusion that follows from the argument

### Weekly output

- Write one complete Task 2 essay in **40 minutes** using at least **250 words**.
- Check the response against the exact question.
- Rewrite the introduction and conclusion if they do not express the same
  position clearly.

- [ ] Week 6 completed

---

## Week 7 — Develop ideas and build an editing routine

### Study

[Unit 8: Writing Task 2 — Checking and correcting](08-writing-task-2-checking-and-correcting.md)

- Section 1: Developing your ideas clearly
- Section 2: Grammatical Accuracy

### Focus

- extend ideas instead of listing unsupported claims
- check subject–verb agreement and tense
- check sentence boundaries and punctuation
- notice repeated personal error patterns

### Weekly output

- Recheck the timed letter from Week 2 and the timed essay from Week 6.
- Create an error log with four columns: original sentence, correction, reason,
  and reusable pattern.
- Rewrite one weak paragraph from each response.

- [ ] Week 7 completed

---

## Week 8 — Assess your writing and take a full mock test

### Study

[Unit 8: Writing Task 2 — Checking and correcting](08-writing-task-2-checking-and-correcting.md)

- Section 3: Assessing your language

### Focus

- assess task response, organisation, vocabulary, and grammar separately
- identify the two weaknesses that most affect clarity or score
- manage the complete 60-minute Writing test

### Weekly output

- Complete one full General Training Writing test:
  - Task 1: **20 minutes**, at least **150 words**
  - Task 2: **40 minutes**, at least **250 words**
- Review both responses and add recurring mistakes to the error log.
- Choose two specific targets for the next month of writing practice.

- [ ] Week 8 completed

---

## After this plan

Continue with one complete 60-minute Writing test each week. Alternate among
informal, semi-formal, and formal letters, and keep updating the same error log.
Spend more review time on Task 2 because it contributes more to the Writing
score.
