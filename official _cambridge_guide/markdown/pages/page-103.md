Writing skills

2 Comparing and contrasting data When you are describing a chart or graph, you are actually describing the patterns in the data. Before you begin to write, spend one or two minutes noticing the different features of the visual information.

2.1 Look at this Writing Task and then answer the questions. The bar chart below shows the net worth of the UK from 2000 to 2010 according to three different sectors.

Test Tip Remember that, to achieve a high score for Writing Task 1, you need to select and report on the main features and make comparisons where relevant.
