### How long is the Speaking paper?

The Speaking test is an interview with an examiner that lasts between 11 and 14 minutes.

#### What will | need to talk about?

The interview has three separate parts and is divided up as follows.

|Part|Timing|What will | need to talk about?|
|---|---|---|
|1|4-5 mins|The examiner will ask you questions about familiar<br>topics (e.g. hobbies, likes and dislikes, daily routine).|
|2|3-4 mins||The examiner will giveyou a booklet showing a<br>topic and some suggestions on it. You need to talk<br>about the topic for 1-2 minutes. You have about one<br>minute to write notes before you begin. The examiner<br>may ask you some follow-up questions.|
|3|4-5 mins|The examiner will ask you more detailed and more<br>abstractquestionslinkedtothetopicinpart2.|


How is the Speaking paper assessed? The examiner will listen carefully to your answers and will assess your speaking according to the following criteria.

|Criteria|Whatdothese criteria mean?|
|---|---|
|Fluency and<br>Coherence|Do you speak in a fluent way that is easy to understand?<br>Do you link your ideas together clearly?|
|Lexical<br>Resource|Can you accurately use or attempt to use a wide range<br>of vocabulary accuratelyand effectively? Can you explain<br>your ideas even if you do not knowa particularword?<br>Can you use or attempt to use higher-level vocabulary<br>accurately?|
|Grammatical<br>Range and<br>Accuracy|Can you use a wide range of grammatical structures<br>|<br>rather than repeating basic structures? Are your<br>sentences accurate, or do you often make grammatical<br>mistakes?|
